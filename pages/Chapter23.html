Chapter 23

What have I done...? What if she doesn't forgive me? What if she never comes back again? Prince was sitting in the room with Anna, panicking.

It's only been a few hours, since Robin left, but he was already falling apart.

The shock of seeing his friend die in front of his eyes still lingered, making him way too attached to her.

He desperately didn't want to lose her again.

Prince, you need to pull yourself together. Anna tapped him on the shoulder. It's Robin we're talking about. She will come back.

How can you be so sure...? What if I made her hate me for the rest of her life? I acted like such a fool...

More importantly, Prince. How is that confession going? Anna was the one who encouraged him to confess his feelings, thinking even if he got rejected, he could at least move on, and not suffer like this.

...she still doesn't believe me... he mumbled.

Ugh, I can't with you two. How were you trying to confess?

Once again, Prince told her about the incident with Tia. Then, his confession after he took Robin to the infirmary. Then, all the days she asked him about his crush and the days he tried to tell her.

Robin really is dense, isn't she...? Anna sighed. Maybe you should just be more direct about it. I'm not saying you're doing anything wrong, but certain people may need certain approaches.

More direct...? Prince muttered.

Yes, more direct. Anna nodded. It's just a little thing, but if I understand correctly, you haven't told her 'I love you.' directly, did you?

But I-!

You casually said 'It's you' when she asked who your crush was. Sure, it doesn't get any clearer than that, but maybe it didn't feel serious enough to her. Maybe she used to have feelings for someone who made fun of her because of it.

Prince clenched his fists, both at the thought of Robin having feelings for someone else but also at the thought of her being made fun of because of it.

How could someone...?

Now, I just said that as an example. Anna shrugged. But the point is, you need to be more explicit about your feelings. Or, if she keeps dodging the question, just move on.

Prince sighted. The though of letting her go was unbearable. But... staying by her side as her friend was still better than losing her completely. She was his most precious friend first and foremost, his feelings for her only came second.

...I understood... But... what do I do if she never comes back?

Well... You'll just have to trust her and give her space. Anna placed a comforting hand on his shoulder. I know she treasures you just as much as you treasure her. So trust your friend and wait for her, okay?

Yeah... Thanks, Anna. You're a lifesaver. Prince said, feeling a bit better.

Now that you've got that sorted out... I need to get going. Anna stood up, gathering her things. Take care of yourself, Prince.

Where are you going? Prince asked curiously, noticing his friend took special care of her appearance.

Oh, I'm going on a date.

A date?!

Robin arrived safely to Miriem Kingdom with Prince Levander.

He helped her get settled in, got her a room, and made sure she had everything she needed.

Then, they spent the next few days in the magic tower, talking to the researchers, comparing ideas and just having fun experimenting with new spells and potions.

Robin was amazed by the advanced magic of the tower. She felt like she was in paradise. She spent hours in the library and the lab, exploring the centuries worth of knowledge.

As the days passed, Robin found herself growing more comfortable in the magical environment. She was grateful for Prince Levander's support and the opportunity to learn from the brilliant minds at the tower.

But she also begun to grow antsy. She'd stare out the window for extended periods of time. Other times, she'd get lost in her thoughts during conversations.

Hey, Robin? Is everything alright? Lev sat her down.

Mhm... yeah, I'm fine. Robin replied dazedly.

Girl, you've been staring out the window for ages! Lev said, concerned.

I just... I don't know. Robin said, looking away. How do you think Prince is doing?

She finally asked.

Prince...? From Cyan? Oh-! A wide grin spread across Lev's face. I didn' know you two were dating!

Robin perked up, her face flushing red.

N-No-! We aren't dating! Robin protested, her cheeks even more flushed.

Really? Lev raised an eyebrow. But he seemes to be on your mind an awful lot for someone you're not dating!

That is... We're friends... Robin mumbled, avoiding Lev's gaze.

Yeah, but you like him, no?

As a friend, sure... Robin replied in an uncertain voice.

As a friend? Lev mused. Or is it more than that?

Stop it, Lev! Robin giggled, her face now fully red. He has someone else he loves!

Hmmm... are you sure about that? Lev questioned. He remembered the hostile way Prince had looked at him when he thought he's gonna take Robin away.

Well... he told me he has someone he likes... but whenever I ask him about it, he jokes around and says it's me.

Lev had to take a pause to process that.

So... He told you he likes you... but you think he's lying?

It just... feels that way. Like he's just joking around just to tell me it was a joke if I take it seriously.

Hmmm... Trust issues much? The prince mumbled to himself. Then, Robin, let's talk about how you feel about him!

Me? He's my precious friend. Robin said with a soft smile.

A precious friend... Lev repeated. Do you hug him often? Hold hands?

...yes... Robin mumbled, her face flushing red again. But that's something friends do too!

And how many friends do you have that you hug often? Hold hands? Lev raised an eyebrow, making Robin pause. How would you feel if that precious friend of yours got a fiancé?

Eh...? The question shocked Robin. Well... if it was someone he likes... and they also like him... then I'd be happy for them! I want him to be happy.

And what if after getting a fiancé, he started putting more distance between you two?

Robin flinched at the thought. She never thought about that possibility.

Why would he do that? Robin asked, her voice small.

Relationships are complicated, Robin. His fiancé may not like him holding hands with you or hugging you. He will have less time to spend with you, too.

That... I don't want that... the fairy mumbled, pouting.

You know, there are all different kinds of love... Lev trailed off, looking thoughtful. It is often complicated, but I hope you'll be able to figure out what your kind of love is.

...thank you, Lev. Robin said softly.

What now? Will you go back to Cyan?

I don't know... Robin mumbled, looking down. After how Prince treated you... It was like he wanted to trap me...

It was certainly an intimidating experience... Lev thought back to his confrontation with the prince. But thinking back, he seemed to be scared of something. Honestly, no idea girl, he's your bestie not mine~

I... He is a good person. Robin said softly.

Do you think what he had done was wrong?

I do.

And was it wrong enough to cost you your friendship... or was it just something you should talk about?

After a moment of silence, Robin spoke up with a determined look.

...I'll go back. I need to talk to him.

It saddens me a bit to have to see you go, but I'll overlook it if you promise to visit often. Lev winked.

Of course, I'd love to keep discussing spells with you some other time too!

Prince was anxiously pacing around at the front gate.

Calm down, Prince. Anna put a hand on his shoulder, stopping him.

But she's coming back! he protested. Ahh... I'm not prepared... What if she hates me...?

Well... we'll know soon enough. She said, looking at the forming teleportation gate in front of them.

Ahh- I'm so nervous! Prince couldn't stay still.

Then, the gate began to glow brightly and a figure emerged from within.

Robin-! he called out excitedly, forgetting all his worries the moment he saw her.

He stepped forward, his heart pounding in his chest. He opened his arms for a hug, but then hesitantly lowered them, not wanting to come off too pushy.

I'm back...! Robin said awkwardly.

Robin, I- I'm so so sorry for what happened... I shouldn't have tried to stop you or go behind your back... I promise it won't happen again! If you decide to move to Miriem, I can just follow you!

Prince?! Anna was baffled by how easily this stupid prince could say he'd leave his kingdom for her.

Don't worry, I'm not going anywhere. Robin said, giving them a reassuring smile.

Wait... Don't tell me-! Will you stay in Cyan?? Will you forgive me...? Prince asked anxiously.

Hmm... Robin replied, looking thoughtful. Yepp, you're forgiven. But I'll truly consider leaving if you pull something like this again.

It won't happen again! Prince exclaimed.

I'm glad to be back... Robin ruffled his hair with a smile. Then, she gave the startled prince a tight hug.

He couldn't believe it. She was really back. And she forgave him. She even gave him a hug.

Unable to keep himself composed, Prince felt his eyes watering. He tightened his hold on Robin, trying to keep some of his composure.

But he just couldn't help it. He couldn't hold back the tears.

Robin held the weeping prince while Anna was trying to calm him down and the knights stared in confusion.

Alongside the fairy, the chaotic atmosphere has returned to Cyan.

Chapter 11

As they returned, Robin headed off to where she had met the "guy from the circus." Obviously, Prince didn't let her go alone.

Some of the pinkish powder from the disappearing trick remained. She collected it carefully, then cleared the air of the rest of it.

How come we haven't fainted yet? Do we even need an antidote?

Oh, I just gave you a quick potion to forcefully keep you awake; I took the same before heading out. It's a temporary measure and will wear off in a few hours, but it's great for emergencies!

Prince was amazed by how effortless it all sounded. Sure, he knew Ethernia from a young age, but she rarely used magic around the children. It was always just the same illusion spells to keep them entertained.

Once Robin was done, they headed back to the hut together.

I'm ready!

Back in Ethernia's hut, Robin returned from the late fairy's workshop with two cups. She handed Prince one and kept the other.

Is THAT the medicine?

Ryat asked skeptically.

No? It's just tea. Do you also want some?

Robin asked as if she were stating the obvious.

Why are you so calm?! Aren't you going to die without that antidote???

Riat was furious.

Meanwhile, Robin offered Prince some candies.

Want some?

Sure.

DON'T IGNORE ME!

Ryat shouted, scared to death because of his friend. Why are they all so nonchalant about this? What is he going to do if something happens to Prince?

It's not like we will die immediately," Robin said, munching on a candy.

And we already took them...

And can you tell me when that happened...?

Riat was so over this bullshit.

Right in front of your eyes~" Robin said cheerfully, but Prince took pity on his friend.

Come on, stop teasing him... The candy was the medicine. You were testing me, weren't you? Whether I really knew Lady Ethernia or not—she always gave children candy-shaped, sweet medicine.

Don't just give someone medicine by asking 'Want some?'! What would you have done if he said 'No thank you'?!

At was furious. Robin gave them a devilish grin.

I would have given him a particularly bitter medicine~

Prince and At shared a worried look as Robin evilly laughed at her scheme.

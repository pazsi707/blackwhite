Chapter 16

Months have passed, and by the time the new knights selection ceremony came, Robin became an integral part of the palace's life.

While she ran errands, she often stopped by to assist people with little spells. Reaching higher areas for the maids when they were cleaning, stopping by the medical office when their hands were full of patients, brewing potions for those who needed them... Overall, she fit right in.

Robin! Prince called out to her.

Robin was giving the gardener some exotic seeds when she noticed the prince running towards her.

Prince! What's up? she bid goodbye to the gardener and turned to her friend.

Want to spar with me? He askd, slightly out of breath, as he stopped in front of her.

Sure! the fairy's eyes lit up.

They headed to the training grounds together, but Prince got caught by an attendant on the way.

Go on without me, I'll catch up in a bit! Prince told Robin after making sure the task he got wouldn't take too long. Can you lead the knights' training till I get back?

Leave it to me! the fairy winked.

By the time Robin arrived, the knights were already present.

Hey everyone! Robin called out. Prince had some stuff to take care of so we'll begin without him!

Her voice was commanding as she looked over them. She saw a couple of new faces. They were probably here because of the selection ceremony.

As she gave the instructions, the new knights exchanged skeptical glances.

I thought the prince was leading the training!

Isn't this the errand girl? I saw her with the maids earlier...

Are they making fun of us?

The older knights looked at them with pity, keeping silent.

Is there an issue? Robin asked, stepping forward. She heard every word.

With all due respect! A braver recruit spoke up. Aren't we supposed to be trained by the prince? I believe a training is meaningless without a proper, qualified instructor.

Is that so? The fairy noted calmly.

Even a senior knight would know better how to train us!

Yes, we want a proper instructor!

Hmm... all of you who are dissatisfied... Step forward. Robin said in a commanding tone.

After a moment of hesitation, one of the new knights stepped forward. Then another. And another.

In the end, most of the new recruits had stepped forward.

Very well. Robin said when no one else seemed to want to join them. All of you. Come at me, all at once. The one who hits me first will get to choose the new temporary instructor. Acceptable?

The one who previously spoke up first, nodded hesitantly.

Let's begin. The fairy hopped in front of them, not even bothering to take out a weapon.

The new knights charged at once.

When Prince arrived, instead of training knights, he saw a crowd of them, standing in a circle around the something he couldn't quite see.

The air was loud with cheers and laughter.

Prince approached curiously, as the senior knights made him space in the circle.

When he finally saw what was going on, he let out a soft chuckle.

That is... a very unique way of training. He tried to bite back a giggle.

In the middle of the circle, the poor young knights were running around, trying to land a hit on the fairy who... looked amused?

Not even breaking a sweat, she kept dodging them with ease. She must have gotten bored, because she started putting stickers on the knights.

Most of them were covered in stickers by now. Many had already given up and were sitting on the ground, just watching the rest struggle.

Prince! Suddenly, the fairy noticed her friend. She skipped towards him excitedly, knocking out the last few knights.

I believe I asked you to lead the training...? Prince laughed softly.

Yeah, but they said they wanted a proper instructor! I even offered they could choose! Robin protested. I can't help it if they can't even hit me once!

Prince looked over the new recruits, his eyes twinkling with amusement and a hint of pity.

Did you learn your lesson? He asked gently.

The knights looked away, embarrassed by their failure.

There's nothing to be ashamed of. Prince said kindly. There will always be an opponent stronger than you. The important part is to learn from your failures. What do you think had gone wrong today?

After a moment of silence, one of the knights spoke up, sounding defeated.

...we underestimated our opponent...

That's right. Prince nodded. Keep that in mind for the future. No matter how harmless someone may seem, it's better to stay cautious than to lose your life over it.

He looked at Robin. Would you mind, my lady?

She gave him a mischievous grin then turned to the knights.

Twenty laps around the castle grounds! She gave the command.

The young knights got up from the ground, groaning. Their seniors patted them on the back encouragingly. They too have already learned their lesson the exact same way before. This is why they had so much fun watching the young ones suffer the same fate.

So... about that spar... Prince said, looking at Robin.

Ready when you are. Robin winked.

As the knights finished their laps, they slowly begun to gather around Prince and Robin, watching them in awe. It was nothing like the one-sided beating they had experienced.

It was as if... they were dancing...? Both moved with a precision, not leaving any openings or showing any signs of exhaustion.

Wait- She could use magic?! The new knights were baffled. Is that why we couldn't win?

Not quite. An older knight spoke up. She never used magic against you.

What?! Then how...?

She can hold her battle even without magic. Look how she's matching the prince's movements. That's the exact way she beat you. The older knight said. Although, without her magic, she'd still lose to the prince.

The young knights watched the prince in admiration. How could he fight a magic user on equal terms?

It's nice to finally see him able to spar with someone.

Commander! the older knight called out as he noticed the approaching figure.

I heard there was a commotion so I came to check. But looks like there was nothing to worry about. Anna said, joining the knights.

Recently, she was starting to grow more and more concerned about the prince. His talent grew so much, others had a hard time matching his pace, so after a while, he ran out of sparring partners.

He would still fight them from time to time, but he kept having to hold back. It was refreshing to see him able to spar with someone on equal terms.

Seeing the two preoccupied with their spar, Anna took over the knights' training.

They had already finished up and left by the time Prince and Robin called for a draw. They sat down in the grass, getting some rest and fresh water.

So, what did they need you for? Robin asked curiously.

Prince's expression darkened. They said princes and princesses from the neighboring kingdoms will be arriving soon... They want me to... he swallowed, not even wanting to say the words. ...They want me to choose a bride...

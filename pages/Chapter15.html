Chapter 15

It was a windy day in Cyan - Prince noted, as he was taking a stroll in town. He liked hanging around there, and the citizens loved him too.

He'd chit-chat with them about everyday things, and hear out any issues they had.

That's the kind of ruler Prince was. One, who wants to listen to as many people as possible and act on their behalf. That's what Robin said she admired about him. And what she wished to protect.

Prince shook his head. He was thinking about Robina again. He should focus on the conversation with the elderly lady in front of him.

Trying his best to focus, suddenly, a flyer flew towards him. A boy was chasing after it.

He caught the piece of paper with ease, it was nothing for a swordsmaster.

Just as he was about to give it back, his eyes landed on the letters written in the corner.

ROBINA

For a moment, he though he simply misread it so he took a closer look.

OPEN LETTER TO MY DEAR FRIEND

I CAME BACK TO THE KINGDOM RECENTLY AND I WANT TO MEET YOU AGAIN! LET'S GO AND RETRIEVE LADY ETHERNIA'S TREASURE TOGETHER! I'LL BE WAITING IN THE FOREST, NEXT SATURDAY AT 11:00. I HOPE WE'LL GET TO MEET AGAIN!

ROBINA

What's... this? Prince was baffled.

Oh, didn't you know about this, Your Highness? The elderly lady asked. It's all over the town recently!

No... I... Excuse me. He gave the paper back to the boy and stormed off, back to the castle.

Indeed, he found multiple of the same flyer on the way. He took one with himself.

It took a while till he found the fairy.

She recently got a workshop where she could work on spells and potions in her free time. Even when Prince barged in, she was tinkering with some leaves and flowers.

Robin, what is this?! He asked frantically.

Oh, you saw it too? I'm looking for a friend whom I used to play with at Granny Ethernia's house.

But this is insane! Who would fill the whole town with flyers just to find a person??

Not just this town, but that's besides the point. Robin said nonchalantly, throwing some extra leaves into her potion.

And you even wrote about 'treasure'?! Prince kept going. Do you know how many fakes that'll bring forth?

Don't worry, I'll recognize him.

And what will you do if he doesn't show up?! Maybe he didn't even see your flyer!

Oh, I flooded the whole town with those, there's no way he missed it. Don't worry, he will show up, I sure of it. Robin exclaimed confidently.

On the day of the announced meeting...

By the time Robin had arrived, a large crowd of people had already gathered by the forest. And among them...

So, you came after all. Robin turned to her friend.

I just didn't want you to get hurt. Prince pretended to be nonchalant but he was clearly worried about Robin and her so-called 'long-lost-friend'.

All right people, let us go! Robin gave out the instruction.

Suddenly, a person spoke up.

I'm the real one! What are all these other people doing here?

Then another.

No, I'm the real one!

Then another...

STOP! Robin shut down the increasing volume. Everyone is coming along! If someone has an issue with it, they can go home!

In the end, no one left and they all headed into the forest together.

...will this be alright? Prince asked Robin. They were walking side by side, chatting.

Don't worry, it'll work out! But Robin's confidence only made Prince more concerned.

After a while, Robin told them they'll take a break.

Everyone settled down, putting up their tents.

Robin stepped away for a moment. When she returned to her tent, she found a note.

LET'S MEET AT THE USUAL PLACE

What's that? Prince read it, leaning over Robin's shoulder.

One of them must have left it for me.

Robin... don't tell me you're...

Of course I'm going! I want to meet my friend, afterall!

Robin arrived to the clearing where she used to meet up with her friend. It felt... nostalgic.

Soon, she heard a rustle from the nearby bushes. A boy, around her age, stepped out from behind the trees.

Were you the one who left the message? Robin asked.

Yes! Robina... I know I can't prove that it's me, well, any better than this... but... He pulled Robina into his arms, lifting her chin up. I liked you ever since we were little... I haven't forgotten about you, even for a moment! Please... could you give us one more chance...?

The sudden confession startled Robina. For a moment, she didn't know what to say. As she was about to answer...

WHA-!? The guy shrieked as a figure jumped down from a tree, forcefully yanking Robin out of his arms.

Who are you-?! Let her go! The man shouted, startled.

The figure was holding onto Robin tightly, in a way that wasn't painful, but she couldn't turn around to see their face, that was hidden by a hood.

...you shouldn't be the one saying that, Mr. Impostor. The figure spoke calmly.

Wha- Who are you to call me an impostor?!

Well, in fact, he's the real one~ Robin said calmly, to the dread of the found out impostor.

But just as the words fell from her lips, she felt the figure's hold on her loosen.

By the time she turned around, the figure was nowhere to be seen.

The impostor also took this opportunity to flee.

Robin stared into the forest where the figure had disappeared to, dejected.

It's kinda unfair, you know... Running away like that... She pouted. Do you hear me, Prince? Come back here!

A person slowly, unsurely stepped out from the bushes. He cast his eyes to the ground, not daring to look up.

...how did you know? Did my... voice give it away...? He asked quietly, sounding defeated.

Don't give me that! You barely changed. I knew from the moment I saw you running away with that bread.

But then... why organize a whole event...?

I wanted to figure out why you pretended not to know me. Robin sat down on a fallen tree trunk, taking a hopeful glance between the boy and the place beside her.

He let out a defeated sigh and sat down beside her. He kept looking down and fiddling with his fingers.

...I didn't want you to come to hate me... He said quietly, in a broken voice.

Why would I hate you?

Because... Prince swallowed and bit his lips. I was the one who killed Ethernia.

You... what? Robin couldn't believe her ears. There no way Prince... What happened?

Prince flinched, hearing the unfamiliar, serious tone directed at him. He glanced away, the words not forming...

I... I...

He was almost nearing a panic attack, when he was suddenly snapped out of it by Robin lowering her head onto his shoulder.

...why? That was all that came out of him.

I don't know what happened between you and her... but I know what kind of person my friend is. And he would never hurt someone without a good reason. So. I trust you.

Even... when you have... no idea- Prince's voice broke as his tears begun falling.

Robin wrapped her arms around him.

It's okay. I'm here. Everything will be alright.

Prince held onto her as the weight of his secret was pulling him down. They remained like that for a while.

When suddenly-

Okay, I cannot bear to watch this any longer

The two of them jumped apart like birds when you throw a stone at them.

Greetings, Robin, I am proud to see how well you've grown up!

Granny?! Weren't you... you know... supposed to be dead? Robin was flabbergasted.

I merely turned into a ghost, you see! But this kid here... He insisted on keeping it a secret from you at any cost. Sorry little one for breaking my promise, but I can't bear to watch this go on any longer.

What... happened? Robin was glancing between the two of them. She was so confused.

Do you wanna tell her or should I? Ethernia asked Prince.

I... Prince's voice choked up once again. He glanced down in guilt. Then glanced up, giving Ethernia a pleading look. He knew he wasn't able to talk about it. But Ethernia was right. Robin had the right to know.

The young fairy looked between them. Then, she took Prince's hand and pulled him back down on the tree trunk. Even as she summoned a couch for Ethernia, she kept holding his hand reassuringly.

I see you've improved a lot, Robin. I'm very proud of you! Her teacher noted as she sat down on the summoned piece of furniture that was able to hold her up, even in her ghostly form.

Robin proudly straightened her back, but seeing Prince still mostly curled up, trying to seem as small as possible, she put her head back down on his shouder, waiting for the explanation.

As Prince bit his lip again, guilt eating him up, Robin reached up. She tapped his lips, making him gasp in surprise.

Don't hurt yourself She said quietly.

Prince gave her a pleading look.

Prince. No matter what it is, it's gonna be alright.

But what if-

No what ifs. Just tell me. That's what friends are for. She reassuringly squeezed his hand.

He swallowed. Then the first words came, in a quiet whisper. Robin didn't ask him to speak up. She didn't rush him. She simply waited and listened.

I... I was such a fool...

Robin squeezed his hand reassuringly, receiving a small, grateful smile in return.

I just... couldn't bear being stuck in the palace anymore... I ran away to the forest... I stole a spell book and some ingredients from Ethernia... He swallowed, a shiver running through his spine as he recalled the memory.

I'm so so sorry... He glanced up at the old fairy, who shook her head.

I already accepted your apologies back then, young Prince. You're still growing, it's natural to make mistakes.

But not mistakes like this... the boy lowered his head.

What were you trying to do with Granny's stuff, Prince? Robin tilted her head curiously.

I... I just wanted to make an invisibility potion... to be able to hide away from people... go wherever I wanted to... He stole a glance at Robin, his ears flushing a bit. Maybe even... find you...

The last part was barely audible. He was ashamed of himself. How could he have acted so selfishly?

That's pretty understandable Robin noted calmly. If I was stuck in that stuffy office of yours for so long, I would have tried to run away too.

But... it's my responsibility as a prince...

Pshh- bullsh- khm. I mean- Robin dodged Ethernia's disapproving glance. What I mean is. Everyone needs a little freedom. Even princes. So-! From today on, I'll take responsibility to kidnap you whenever you feel like things are getting too much! Robin excaimed proudly.

Kidnap me...? Prince let out a small, baffled chuckle. She was the only one who could say something so outrageous with such confidence. He truly missed her.

Ethernia watched the two interact with a contented smile, waiting a moment before speaking up.

Now, Robin... Can you guess what went wrong with the potion? She quizzed her student.

He must have used moonflowers instead of white evening primroses, right? The young fairy replied with confidence.

That's right. Ethernia praised her student. And that's why the potion didn't work as expected.

Prince dropped his gaze to the ground, feeling the weight of his mistake.

He still remembered the explosion after he added the last ingredients. It was naive of him to think he could make the potion without proper training. Ethernia managed to push him aside in the last moment, but got caught in the explosion.

But why did you want to keep it from me? Spells go wrong all the time. Robin asked her friend, unable to understand his reasoning.

But... not like this... Your only family is hanging between life and death because of me! He burst out.

To his surprise, Robin let out a chuckle.

That is... being a bit overdramatic, no? She giggled. Oh noooo! Hanging between life and death!

Prince was flabbergasted. How could she joke about this?

I tried telling him too but he wouldn't listen. Ethernia sighed.

Wha- Hey! Y-You're basically a ghost! Why is neither of you taking this seriously? Prince was in a shock.

You know, I guess, reality is a bit more bendy for faires. Robin said, pondering how to explain. Like, this is just a simple potion, that can be reversed once we have the right ingredients. Nothing grand or ground-breaking.

Are you... not worried at all? About not being able to undo this? Prince asked timidly.

Who do you think we are? Robin asked with a proud grin. We're the most powerful fairy and her teacher~! These's no way something minor like this could pose an issue!

The most powerful fairy AND her teacher? Ethernia repeated, raising an eyebrow. Wouldn't it be... 'The most powerful fairy and her student'?

Eh~ Don't worry about the specifics~ Robin said with a mischievous grin in her eyes.

That's just how fairies are. Carefree and proud. Thinking they are the strongest. Invincible, even.

Will you return to being Cyan's guardian fairy after we finished the new potion? Robin asked her teacher.

Chapter 19

It was early in the morning but Prince was awake and in a rush. He couldn't believe what had happened.

He was told Robin had attacked Princess Rosalia out of jealousy. There's no way... Although, he wouldn't have minded if Robin was jealous over him... but... that's not who Robin was.

Last night, a little after the fairy had departed, Princess Rosalia has somehow been informed about the situation and offered Divia's advanced transportation system to help move a larger amount of knights to help. She even offered some of her own knights...

Tia told me to be careful with Rose, but I didn't listen... What could have happened? Prince still had no idea about the situation but he was worried for the fairy.

The reporting knight told him Robin attacked Rosalia's knights because she felt like they were taking her job... then attacked Princess Rose because she was jealous of how close she had gotten to Prince.

Robin...? Territorial like that...? If only... Prince sighed.

The moment he learned they had taken her to the dungeon to an anti-magic cell, he rushed there without thinking.

Robin-! Panting from the exhaustion, he arrived to her cell.

The fairy was sitting on the sole metal plate in the room, presumably a bed. She looked exhausted, her body still covered in injuries, no one even considered treating her.

...Prince? She looked up slowly, surprised.

Robin! What have they done to you...? He unlocked the door in spite of the guards' protests and helped her up. Let's go, heal you up.

No... It's okay... Won't you get in trouble for this? Robin asked, concerned. But her prince was already pulling her out of the cell.

What kind of person would I be for not treating an injured friend?

Knowing there was no changing his mind, Robin followed after him.

The guards tried to stop him from taking the fairy out of the anti-magic cell, but they had no chance in face of the prince's determination.

While the healers were working on Robin's injuries, Prince looked over her with concern.

...what happened there...? He asked softly.

The bandits turned out to be Divian knights. The princess injured herself saying I did it, then I was brought back and locked up. Robin said nonchalantly.

How dare they...! Prince clenched his fists. Don't worry, I'll get you out of there.

Robin gave him a soft smile and ruffled his hair.

Yes, yes~ I'm counting on you~

For the rest of the treatment, Prince stayed quiet, almost as if lost in thought.

When the healers finished, the fairy and the prince thanked them and left.

In the empty corridor, they walked in silence for a while. Suddenly, Prince stopped.

Noticing, Robin stopped with a curious expression. Seeing his troubled expression, she leaned against the wall with a smile.

Is everything okay, Prince?

Robin... do you remember when I said there was someone I liked? Someone that wasn't Tia. Prince said hesitantly.

Yeah, what about it? Wait- Will you tell me who it is? Robin asked excitedly.

Prince took a deep breath and put his hand against the wall beside her, leaning a hint closer.

It's... it's you. Prince said with a serious expression, blushing furiously.

A pause. Then...

Robin burst out laughing. Yeah, sure~ she stepped away from the wall, taking a few steps down the corridor, her back towards the prince. Thank you, Prince. You said that to cheer me up, didn't you?

Prince was taken aback by her response, his face flushing red. In the end, he couldn't find the words to respond. They returned to her cell in silence.

I'll get you out of here. I promise. That was all he could say before the guards ushered him away.

But the next day, Prince was unable to return to the fairy's side. Every time he went to the dungeon, the guards would stop him on orders of his parents.

It was the same the next day. He returned to his room in the evening, disappointed. Then, he noticed something in his window.

After Prince took Robin back to the cell from the healers, she decided to rest. To her, the next day was slightly more eventful than to the prince who was unable to see her.

Turns out, her punishment has been decided. Charged with the attempted murder of royalty, her punishment was going to be... death... by hanging. The princess, allegedly feeling uneasy, asked for the punishment to be executed immediately. They scheduled it for the next evening.

Robin was quiet as they broke the news to her. She had but one request. She asked to be burned... instead of hanged.

But... even this one request has been denied of her. They feared she'd pull some kind of trick.

As the time for the execution arrived, guards came into the fairy's cell and put anti-magic handcuffs on her wrists. They led her outside, to the palace courtyard.

It was an open space, to where anyone from the town could come and watch. Many worried faces gathered. After all, they had all taken a liking to the fairy who kept visiting them alongside the prince, helping them out however she could.

The sky was dark, the only light coming from the guards' torches illuminating the fairy's face.

As they neared the execution site, Robin suddenly tore a torch from one of the guards' hands and jumped on a stack of wood to oversee all the gathered.

This was the moment Prince had witnessed from his window. No one told him about the execution. But he knew the area where they took Robin. He knew it was only used for executions.

He tried to rush there but the door to his room has been locked from the outside, two knights standing guard. Even the door to the neighboring room was blocked.

His room was so high up, he would've died if he had jumped. But its height also gave him perfect view of what was happening.

Down there, Robin has jumped onto the stack of wood, holding the torch high above her head, getting the attention of everyone present.

The knights looked at each other. None of them dared to move. They all felt it was unfair. They wanted to let her at least get this moment. Her chains prevented her from using magic anyways so they didn't think it would be an issue.

After looking around slowly, she spoke up.

Hey, everyone! I know this is a bit sudden... but don't try to avange me or anything, alright? She looked around with her usual grin. I hope y'all can continue with your lives and not let this get you down.

Before anyone could've reacted, the torch fell from her hands hitting the wood beneath her, making it flare up round her.

Prince watched in horror as the flames engulfed Robin.

NO! He shouted.

No one but the guards by his door could hear his outcry.

They had to rush in to stop the prince trying to jump from his window.

He was trashing around as they held him down, trying to break free, giving the two knights some trouble, but they were able to pull him back safely.

He fell on the ground, sobbing. The guards looked at each other, feeling helpless as the prince kept weeping on the floor heartbreakingly.

The knights at the execution site were similarly helpless. The flames were so large, they had no chance of getting close, to get the fairy out.

By morning, the flames have died down. What remained was a pile of ashes, bones and the scent of smoke.

Prince woke up, feeling exhausted. It... didn't feel real. It didn't feel right. How could they... To her fairy... To Robin...

His throat tightened again as the images of yesterday flooded his mind. The way she stood there, smiling... the fact he was there, treating her wounds just a few days ago... then the image of fire consuming her...

The sudden pain of the memory hit him like a wave. He had to sit down and try to compose himself.

Getting dressed, he left his room, looking like a shadow of his former self. As he mindlessly strolled the corridors, he passed by the palace's main entrance where a commotion drew his attention.

The guards at the entrance were arguing with a boy who desperately wanted to get inside.

What is going on? He asked, exhausted.

It is nothing of importance, Your Highness. One of the guards replied.

Take me to the prince! I need to talk to him! The loud boy insisted.

Shut up! You can't just barge in here! The guard snapped.

Wait. Prince waved down the guards. Yes? What is it you have to say to me?

Are you the prince? The boy asked.

Yes, I am. Prince kneeled down in front of the boy. How can I help you?

The fairy told me to give this to the prince. Only to the prince! The boy exclaimed proudly, handing over a small, glowing orb. It took me some time to get here, but here I am!

Ah... are you from that village? The one Robin was protecting...? Prince recalled the fairy's mission.

Yes! She helped us a lot! And she gave me this mission! Oh... but it's supposed to be a secret! The boy whispered.

Thank you... Prince took the orb from him. The village was days of normal travel away, so the boy's story made sense.

The moment the prince touched the orb, a bright projection appeared in the air before them.

It took them a while to process what they were seeing.

Prince felt his heart sink. This was... Robin's proof...

The projection showed everything that had happened in front of the village that day. The boy told him the fairy gave him the job to look for this orb in the hollow of a tree beside the road after she's gone. She told him that if he finds it, he should bring it to the prince. She insisted that he has to give it to the prince directly.

Prince didn't quite process most of what the boy was saying. He was in a shock. They killed Robin over... this. Over a lie...

...Princess Rosalia. It was all her fault.

Furious, he headed to the throne room to confront his parents.

How could you have done this to her?! She was innocent!

She attacked the princess. If we didn't deal with her, Divia could've declared war on us.

But Princess Rosalia was lying!

Prince... Sometimes... you have to make difficult decisions. You'll understand with age. We're truly sorry for what happened to your friend.

Liars-! Prince stormed out of the room, not wanting to let them see his tears.

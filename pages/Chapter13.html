Chapter 13

As weeks passed by, Anna kept leaving for work during the day, leaving Robin alone in the house.

Apparently, she is a royal knight and trains in the castle every day - something, Robin has a hard time believing.

In most countries she had seen before, commoners couldn't just go and join the royal guard. But, she had to acknowledge - Anna had a strong build and fluid movement.

Robin had no doubt her friend was a talented swordmaster. But working in the palace still felt unreal.

During these days, Robin would go out on walks to cure her boredom. This was one of those days.

I mean, he IS the Prince of Cyan... just not 'Prince' as a title but Prince as a name... Robin mumbled to the crows she was feeding.

She used to feed pigeons too, but there has been a certain incident. She saw a pigeon hop onto another one's back.

Aww, they're playing, so cute! She told someone. And... said person enlightened her about what the birds were actually doing. Since then, she thinks pigeons are disgusting creatures and keeps her distance from them.

She got goosebumps as she recalled the memory.

Crows are so much better... such smart creatures...

One of the crows hopped to her with a flyer in it's beak. They sometimes do that. Bring stuff, that is.

Robin took the paper and pet the bird, giving it some extra seeds.

Yepp, crows are the best. Well, after foxes. She pondered. They don't try to lie to you about...

Robin didn't even notice she stopped mumbling as she got lost in thought. It seemed like the flyer had given her an idea.

Eventually, she gave the birds the last batch of crumbs and headed off towards the palace, slipping the flyer into her pocket.

It wasn't like she was going there to meet Prince.

He did say his house was around that area.

But if she happened to bump into him, it would of course be a total coincidence. Yepp, she just happened to walk this way. With no specific person in mind.

As she was strolling the streets, looking around not suspiciously at all, she decided to take a closer look at the palace since she was already here. It was a really pretty building, they said many famous masters worked on it. Not as if Robina knew any of their names.

She walked closer, admiring the sparkly walls when she noticed - the main entrance was wide open! She couldn't believe her eyes. Who leaves a palace door open?!

She decided to take a peek. Well, maybe explore a bit too. It's not her fault the door was open... and she could get some evidence that Prince is not part of the royal family.

Just as she stepped inside, a dagger flew her way, grazing her face. A drop of blood fell as the dagger hit the door behind her.

She looked around. The large hall was filled with all kinds of people fighting each other relentlessly.

She had no idea what was going on but it seemed like no one would notice if she joined in. And we all know a bored fairy doesn't think twice about such an opportunity.

Robina jumped into the middle of the fight, carefreely dodging attacks and handing out mischevious enchantments. Making a person's sword floppy, another's punches soft, a third's weapons flowers... small things many may miss, and no one could connect back to her.

When she had enough fun, she got out of the crowd onto the other side, jumping on a sword, over a mace, safely landing on the ground while dodging a bomb flying by.

A fight like this was easy for her. She had her magic, sure, but she also studied all kinds of different combat styles and methods while she was travelling the world.

But even great fighters can be surprised.

We have a WINNER! Thank you all for participating, you may leave now.

A weird figure lifted up the confused Robin's hand in triumph. She glanced around, trying to make sense of this mess.

She was standing on some kind of podium, surrounded by the dejected faces of the previously so energetic fighters. Some still grumbled, others already headed for the exit.

Suddenly, she felt a strong bloodlust coming from the crowd. She swung her head but by the time she looked, the feeling was gone.

Before she had any more time to process the situation, the weird man begun pulling her along.

Follow me, brave warrior!

Unable to react from the surprise, Robina allowed herself to be dragged away.

The weird man kept talking about some selection process or whatnot, but Robin was way too confused to listen.

Go ahead!

Robin was snapped out of her thoughts when the weird man came to a halt in front of her. He swiftly opened a door, and ushered her inside.

Curious, she entered the room.

But just as she was about to ask what was going on, the door slammed shut behind her.

...seriously? She thought.

Then, she heard a familiar voice.

Robin...?

What?! Prince?

Once again, she found herself baffled. What was her friend doing here? And why were they closed in this room?

What are you doing here, Prince? Have you been caught trying to steal again...? Robin asked, concerned.

That's what I should be asking you! How did you even get in here??

Well... the door was open... so I came in to prove you're not the prince... But then I ran into these fighting creeps...! ...They wanted to fight, so I granted their request... aand then a weird guy brought me here... And that's how I got here! Robin summed up her afternoon adventure.

ARE YOU AN IDIOT?! Prince lashed out. Don't just follow random people to places! And why would you join a fight without thinking??? What if something had happened to you?!

Prince. The fairy interrupted. Do I really seem that weak? She asked, annoyed.

I never said you were weak. But you're not invincible either. He said in a calm, authoritative tone.

He noticed the scratch on Robin's cheek. Without realizing, he reached out to touch it.

Ah- It's nothing! Robin jumped back, flustered. She quickly lifted her hand, cast a healing spell. See? I can easily heal something like this!

For a moment, Prince stared at his hand that was left hanging in the air. There was but a hint of disappointment in his eyes.

Anyways... Robin said, breaking the silence. What are YOU doing here?

I told you I'm the crown prince, remember? Prince explained in a calm tone. This is supposed to be a competition, to get me a strong personal guard. Anyone in the kingdom could enter. First, we choose the strongest competitor, then test their strength against mine.

Wait, you have to fight the winner??

Oh! That was actually my idea! I didn't really want a guard following me around everywhere, so I proposed that if they were weaker than me, I could protect myself better without them.

ARE YOU AN IDIOT?! Robin said, exasperated.

What...? Prince asked, confused. He was pretty proud of his plan.

Fine, I'll show you. Robin said in a cold voice, summoning two daggers. You said I had to fight you, didn't you? Let's fight then.

I don't know what you are up to, but guess I'll just have to wait and see, huh? Prince unsheathed his sword confidently, ready for battle. Let's fight.

As he saw Robin move, he also lunged forward. They fought pretty evenly for a while.

Then, Robin jumped into the air. Prince tried to catch her in motion but she simply jumped over his sword, floating in the air for a moment.

Before he could react, she was already behind him, pointing a dagger at his throat.

What would you do if I sliced your neck right now? She asked with a cold anger in her tone.

I know you'd never do that. He answered, his voice calm and steady.

YOU'D DIE! Robin cried out. What if, instead of me, an assassin came just to kill you?! What would I do if...?

Prince, interrupted her outburst, wrapping his arms around her, pulling her into a tight hug.

Thank you. For worrying for me. He said gently.

Robin embarrassedly pulled back after staying in his embrace for what felt like a moment too long.

You should worry about yourself too. She told her friend, trying to look nonchalant, pretending to get some non-existent dust off her clothes.

I will. He replied in a gentle voice, a bit disappointed that the hug didn't last any longer. With a hint of excitement in his voice, he asked Does this mean you'll stay here in the palace?

Hmm... I guess so? Robin pondered. It's better than trusting you to a random person.

Prince's eyes lit up.

Then-! Let me show you around! He took her hand eagerly and led her out of the room.

He showed her every nook and cranny, even his carefully hidden secret passages no one knew about. Then, he stopped in front of a large door.

What-? I'll get my own room? Robin asked, her eyes wide with surprise as Prince ushered her in excitedly.

Yes! It's right beside mine! For... safety reasons... He said sheepishly.

Woah-! It's huge! How is the bed so soft?? And the window is so big too! Where does this lead to? She looked around in awe, stopping in front of a door standing on the left side of the room.

Oh- That... Prince fumbled over his words. It leads to my room... It's on the other side of the wall... So you could... protect me... in case anything happens...? He was trying so hard to hide the blush creeping up his cheeks.

Neat! Robin exclaimed, her eyes sparkling with excitement, completely missing her friend's embarrassment. Soo... When do I start?

Whenever you're ready. He replied with a warm smile, his cheeks still burning a little. You can take your time moving in...

He couldn't even finish the sentence, Robin has already summoned all her belongings. For a few minues, various items were floating around in the room around her.

Princed watched in awe as the fairy effortlessly settled in. When she decided everything was in place, she swung around to face Prince.

So... do I start now? She asked with a mischievous grin.

Prince let out a surprised chuckle.

If you say you're ready...

The rest of the day passed in an instant. Prince told Robin about her tasks while the fairy more of less listened.

...do you really have no intention of becoming the guardian fairy of Cyan...? Prince asked cautiously, after they went over the last administrative matters too.

Sorry. Even if it's you, I won't sign the contract. We never know, something might happen to you. Then, I'd be tied to another person for my entire life. And I'm not up for that.

Prince nodded understandingly. He didn't want her to get tied down like that either. He decided to drop the matter entirely.

He spent the rest of the evening helping Robin settle in, making sure she had everything she needed.

So... see you tomorrow? Prince asked, a hint of hope in his voice.

See you tomorrow! Robin replied with a confident grin.

Chapter 18

Next morning, Prince went to see Tia off early in the morning.

How did it go with Ryat? I'm really sorry about Robin... Prince apologized for the fairy's meddling.

Eh, he turned out to be not my type... Tia shrugged. And don't worry about your little guardian, she was really fun to talk to... I truly get why you like her.

Prince's face flushed in a crimson hue. As they became friends, he let Tiara in on his secret. This was the reason he was blushing when Robin saw them together too.

Unlike her looks, Tia was a more hands-on, getting dirty kind of person. She loved fixing things and preferred overalls over dresses. This was her secret she shared with the prince in return.

Have a safe trip home. Said Prince, slightly worried after seeing the incidents around her in the past few days.

Don't worry, I'll be fine. Tia reassured him with a smile. She had a good reason to be confident.

There you are.

After Prince bid farewell, and returned to the castle, Tiara went to see someone before leaving.

What do I owe this pleasure? The other person asked, their voice dripping with disdain.

You can stop with the tricks. I'll be leaving soon anyway. Tiara said, her voice firm. He never even liked me, it's really unnecessary to target everyone that talks to him.

I have no idea what you're talking about.

...whatever suits you. But you should back away now. You have no idea what you're getting into. You can't beat HER.

We'll see about that.

Tia sighed. I said what I wanted. Now, it's up to you.

...and then do you know what she said? Robin was upsetly telling Anna about the previous day.

Yes? What did she say? Anna chuckled. She found Robin's story amusing, considering she was well aware of Prince's crush on the fairy.

She said she had a crush on Ryat! Like... what is there even to like about that guy?! When she has Prince right in front of her! She protested.

She... she said that? Anna asked, startled. That she likes At?

Yes! Unbelievable, right?! But, I was kind enough to introduce them. Robin said, still mad about it.

Yes... unbelievable... Anna muttered, clearly in disbelief. In a slightly different kind than her friend was in. Sorry Robin... I think I have to go...

Is everything okay...? Robin tilted her head, concerned.

Yeah... yeah, nothing's wrong. She said, trying to reassure her friend. But the way she rushed away made Robin even more worried.

When Robin ran into Anna the next day, she expected her friend to still be feeling down. Surprisingly, Anna was nothing like when she left the day before.

She was upbeat and cheerful, almost seemed to be shining from happiness.

But before Robin could try to lobby for what had happened, she's been called to the administration office.

She's been given a mission. Since many foreign guests were already leaving, they decided they could handle Prince's safety without her for a while.

Robin was starting to get used to helping out with more and more tasks besides guarding the prince - regardless, she still always kept an eye out for with magic. Meanwhile, the administrative staff wanted to test her to make sure the prince was in good hands.

There was a smaller village on the outskirts of the kingdom, near Divia's border, that needed help. Recently, bandits have been attacking the arriving merchants, causing a shortage of supplies for the villagers.

Robin could shorten the days of travel the knights would have needed to a matter of hours.

As she got there, she was greated by the villagers. They were grateful for any help, and welcomed her with open arms.

She had just enough time to prepare before the bandits arrived. The villagers fled to their homes as Robin stood on the road between the village and the bandits.

The bandits approached, their weapons gleaming in the sunlight. They were taunting her with threats of attacking the villagers.

Regardless, it was an easy fight for the fairy. The bandits kept coming, with numbers that seemed to never end, but she kept taking them down and throwing them on a pile.

By the time she disarmed all of them, she's been completely exhausted. Now, that she had a moment to breathe, she took a glance at their uniforms that seemed way too clean for ordinary bandits.

To her surprise, on the uniforms, she saw a crest she recognized. It was... from Divia-

CLAP. CLAP. CLAP.

Robin shot her head up at the sound, her body becoming tense once again.

What she saw, startled her. It was Divia's first princess, Princess Rosalia... But... wasn't she supposed to be in the palace of Cyan, on the event?

Princess Rosalia, being from a neighboring kingdom, was one of the last ones to leave the gathering.

For some reason, Robin never liked her. She felt... dangerous.

Oh, no! My knights! How could you...? Suddenly, the princess's face morphed from a confident grin to a terrified expression.

Another group of knights were approaching. They were... from Cyan...?

Princess Rosalia falls on the ground, her eyes wide with fear. Before the Cyan knights could see, she injured herself with a rock multiple places.

When the knights arrive, their eyes widen. They see the fairy standing there, covered in dirt and sweat, in front of her is the proud princess sitting of the ground, seemingly scared and injured, her knights all beaten up.

After the shock wore off, the Cyan knights approached the fairy with suspicion. One knight took the bravery to approach her, sword pointed at her. They surrounded her with caution.

Seeing the princess shooting her a mocking smile behind the knights' backs, she was now certain this was a trap. To avoid more serious trouble, she followed them without resistance.

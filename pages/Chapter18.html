Chapter 18

Next morning, Prince went to see Tia off early in the morning.

How did it go with Ryat? I'm really sorry about Robin... Prince apologized for the fairy's meddling.

Eh, he turn out to be not my type... Tia shrugged. And don't worry about your little guardian, she was really fun to talk to... I truly get why you like her.

Prince's face flushed in a crimson hue. As they became friends, he let Tiara in on her secret. This was what Robin saw when she was watching them together and she saw him blush.

Unlike her looks, Tia was a more hands-on, getting dirty kind of person. She loved fixing things and preferred overalls over dresses. This was her secret she shared with the prince in return.

Have a safe trip home. Said Prince, slightly worried after seeing the incidents around her in the past few days.

Don't worry, I'll be fine. Tia reassured him with a smile. She had a good reason to be confident.

There you are.

After Prince said his goodbyes, and returned to the castle, Tiara went to see someone before leaving.

What do I owe this pleasure? The other person asked, their voice dripping with disdain.

You can stop with the tricks. I'll be leaving soon anyway. Tiara said, her voice firm. He never even liked me, it's really unnecessary to target everyone that talks to him.

Chapter 24

The next few days passed ...surprisingly normally.

It was as if nothing had happened, Prince went back to do his tasks, Robin to guard him.

There were two things that changed, however.

One of them was Prince. He started gifting all kinds of things to the fairy. Even now, he got her a necklace he made. It even had a tiny crown on it.

Prince had a hobby of making crowns himself, so he already possessed most skills and tools for creating other kinds of jewelery too. The fairy always enjoyed watching him tinker.

She'd frequently visit his workshop, only to linger and stare.

The other change was Robin. She seemed to have started to become conscious of Prince...?

She begun noticing when their hands brushed against each others', the way he was looking at people, the way he was looking at ...her...

The fairy was skipping by happily in the garden, showing off the necklace Prince made for her to the birds and spirits.

What a stupid sight

Robin paused. She slowly turned to face the owner of the voice.

What do you-

But before she could react, the maid tore the necklace from her hands and threw it into the bottomless fountain lake standing beside them in the palace garden.

It was actually a lake that was so deep no one has ever been able to reach its bottom. Myths said ferocious creatures lived at its depths. For some reason, someone thought it was a good idea to build a fountain on the edge of it.

But the medal fell deep. Robin tried to reach after it but she wasn't fast enough. It disappeared into the darkness.

By the time she could turn to the maid to question her, she was already gone. What was even more offputting is that Robin didn't recognize her face.

She ended up returning to her room with a sigh.

As night fell, she got up from her bed and quietly left her room.

With purposeful steps, she strolled through the loggia on the inner side of the castle wall, heading towards the open balcony in the tower.

She felt the midnight wind on her face as she arrived. Without hesitation, she jumped up onto the ledge of the balcony.

Taking a deep breath, she leaned forward, ready to fall-

ROBIN!! Just as she was about to topple forward, Prince grabbed her arm and pulled her back on the balcony. WHAT WERE YOU THINKING?! WHY ARE YOU JUMPING OFF THE BUILDING IN THE MIDDLE OF THE NIGHT?? DO YOU WANT TO DIE?!

Robin was startled by Prince's sudden appearance and outburst.

He must have overheard the fairy leaving her room and decided to follow in case something was wrong with Robin.

For a moment, the fairy stared at him from the floor. Then, she slowly got up.

...a maid took the necklace you made me and threw it into the bottomless lake... I just wanted to get it back... the fairy explained, feeling a bit guilty for scaring the prince.

Wanted to get it back by jumping from here?!

Look down! We're exactly above the lake... you used a special gem for the necklace that reflects moonlight... see? I can see it from here because of that!

That's not the point! Prince grabbed her shoulders. Why would you do something so dangerous over a mere necklace?!

It's not just a necklace! You put your heart and effort into it! It is something precious to me!

Prince's heart skipped a beat. Seriously... how can she be so lovely...

The moonlight illuminated their features as they stood on the balcony.

Prince put his palm over Robin's mouth and slowly leaned forward, kissing the other side of his hand.

I love you, Robin.

He talked in a gentle, certain voice.

Then, he stepped past the fairy and jumped.

Robin's legs gave out and she slumped onto the ground.

What just happened... he raised a hand to her lips, still in shock.

Then, she heard the splash of Prince landing in the lake.

Startled by the sound and the realization that the prince had jumped, she hurriedly got up, and tried to jump after him.

But her legs were still not functioning properly. One of her ankles got caught in the railing and she fell in the water head first, the impact punching the air out of her lungs.

Prince was already diving deep when he heard the loud spash and saw the fairy sink.

He immediately swam up to her. He held her and scrambled out of the water with her.

COUGH- COUGH-

Robin coughed up some water. She could finally breathe again, but she could still hear her heart beating loudly from the shock.

Except... it wasn't her heart.

Prince was still holding her in his arms. He was soaking wet.

What were you thinking?! How could you jump down like that?! He cried out.

Wha- You jumped first! Robin protested. Right, the necklace-! She turned back to the water, panicked.

But Prince held her before she could jump back in.

He took her hand and placed the rescued object on her palm.

You... how... Robin couldn't believe her eyes. He got her out of the water AND he got the necklace...?

It's okay if I do it, but... I can't bear the thought of you getting hurt... He lowered his head on Robin's shoulder, who squished his cheeks between her palms and lifted his head back up to face her.

I'm also worried-! You can't just go around doing dangerous stuff like that either!

But Robin, I-

You're not the only one who-! The only one who... Robin's face turned red. Then, she leaned forward and... kissed him.

Prince's breath hitched, his heart beat out of his chest. What was happenning?!

Robin slowly pulled back, now both of their faces shining in a crimsom hue.

Robin...

I-! I also... love you... she interrupted the prince. I was thinking... and I... I want you to be happy... but... if possible... I want to be the one making you happy... and stay by your side... so... that is... if you accept me-

Without letting her finish, Prince jumped on the fairy, wrapping his arms tightly around her. The impact made her fall back, the prince landing on top of her.

They looked at each other for a split second, then both burst out laughing.

They stayed like that for a short while, until it started getting cold.

Robin had sprained her ankle when she fell, so Prince lifted her up in his arms and carried her back to her room.

It was an amusing sight. A prince carrying a fairy, both drenched and laughing. Fortunately, they didn't run into anyone. On the way.

When they got back to their rooms, they both took a warm bath and went to bed in their own rooms, promising to spend tomorrow together.

Chapter 25

Did you really mean it from the very beginning...? That you like me?

Robin and Prince were sitting in the park, eating ice-cream.

I never lied about that. But... there was one thing... Prince hesitated.

What is it? Robin tilted her head curiously.

That... when I told you that I liked Tia... that was a lie the prince's face flushed red as he continued. I... I liked you since way before... even before you left Cyan all those years ago... I just wasn't in a position to tell you... you shone so brightly... I didn't want to be the one to hold you back and dim your light... but then... you came back... and I fell in love with you even more... but I felt too guilty about Ethernia to say it... then... I simply got scared... I didn't want to lose you over this... Because of my cowardice, I lied and said it was Tia that I liked... but... it was you, all along... I-I'm sorry, I know I'm just making excuses, I should've been honest with you from the start...

Robin shook her head.

No, it's not your fault... you had your circumstances. And, I'm sorry too. About not believeing you.

Prince lowered his head on Robin's shoulder.

What about you...? You... you said you love me too, but... you're not forcing yourself to say it, right...? He askes anxiously.

That... Robina hesitated. I'm not sure, honestly... Everyone is saying that if you're in love, it should feel obvious, but... I never had that...

Not even as a child...?

Wait, children truly fall in love...? Robin was taken aback. I thought everyone was just saying it as a game...

What?? No way! I liked you since we were this small-! Prince put his hand above the ground as an estimation.

Weird. That aside... you asked if I loved you... I have no idea... I... I feel strongly about you. I want to stay by your side, I want to see you laugh, I want to see you cry, I want to be your strength... but... the special feeling is not there... the butterflies or whatnot... the one everybody is talking about...

...how can you be sure it's not there...?

Honestly, I don't think I can be sure... it's difficult to tell whether you feel something when you're not sure what it should feel like... But... I think if I truly felt it, I wouldn't be questioning it so much.

Robin looked up at the sky thoughtfully.

Then... how do you feel about... us? Prince asked hesitantly.

I... I love you... but probably in a somewhat different way than you love me... I don't have a big, intense feeling... I just have... a quiet appreciation... I like being by your side... and I want to stay even if things get difficult... I love you... like I love my family...? Maybe a bit more. You're my friend, my family, seeing you happy makes me happy too... But... is that enough...? She hesitated. Would you be alright with a partner who doesn't love you properly...?

Who defines what a proper love should look like? Prince leaned back on the bench. To me... if you say you want this... that is all I that matters.

Thank you, Prince. Robin's lips curved into a grateful smile.

After a few minutes of comfortable silence, the fairy spoke up.

So... now that we're dating... will it be different than it was before...? Between us. She asked nervously.

Would you like it to be?

...no. I just... I want to keep being around you like I used to be... I want to keep doing the same things... but I'm not sure... I guess... I'm scared... of losing what we always had...

Robin... Prince sat up, leaning closer. I... I don't want to lose what we have either. I don't think anything needs to change just because we're dating.

But then... how is that different from being friends...?

Well... Prince's face flushed. Maybe now I'll get to hold your hand more often...? And kiss you on the lips...? Oh... are you alright with these things though...? You said you love me a bit differently, if these things make you uncomfortable-

No, no! I... actually like holding hands. And... you taste sweet.

The boy's face turned crimson.

H-How can you say that s-so casually?!

Say what...? Robin tilted her head.

Ahhh... what am I gonna do with you... he nuzzled his face into her neck. ...Robin?

Hmm?

If I ever do something that makes you uncomfortable... you have to tell me, alright...?

Sure~! Robin nuzzled into Prince's hair, now that it was so close to her face. She loved his scent more than the flowers' around them.

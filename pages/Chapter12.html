Chapter 12

A week went by and Robin moved in to her childhood friend's house.

Her name was Anna. They both learned magic from Ethernia, but Anna didn't finish her studies. She knows how to use healing spells but that's basically it. She isn't a full-fledged fairy, she can simply use a few spells, unlike most people.

On the other hand, since Robin had finished her studies, she had to go find a country that didn't have a fairy that time. She travelled a lot and learned countless new spells but couldn't find a place she liked, until Ethernia's letter came.

On the night she bumped into Prince, who was busy stealing bread, she went to speak with Anna, to ask her if she could live with her for the time being. Well, only after showing Prince the way to his run-down house, near the palace. He insisted he knew the way but when he started walking the opposite direction, Robin decided it'd be better to tag along. To his defense, the place they met was a small, abandoned cottage on the outskirts of town, far from the area he said his house was.

They were soo annoying! And soo full of themselves! Robin complained.

'Here, I'll give you money, so do what I say'!

'Ohh we need you to win a war'!

'No gem can be match for your beauty, but here, I'll give you a thousand'!

'I'll make all your wishes come true'!

'If you don't help, I'll make you regret'!

'You should be happy to be able to work for ME'

Aghhh... How can ANYONE be that spoiled?! And there's not only one of them... Why are they rulers?!

Looks like you've been through a lot... Anna chuckled at her friend's outburst. They were chatting in her living room after Robin has made herself comfortable in the guest room.

But you'll like the prince here. She added reassuringly.

Well, if you say so... Robin said sceptically.

You couldn't be less convinced... But he'll prove you wrong. Anna smiled.

DING DONG ran the doorbell.

He's here! Anna said, stepping to the door excitedly.

Who's here...? Robin followed, confused.

Let me introduce you to the prince of Cyan! Anna said proudly, opening the door.

Robin?!

Prince?!

The two stared at each other in disbelief.

What, you already knew each other? That makes things easier! Anna ushered them both back into the living room.

After the initial shock wore off, Robin sighed.

What a shame... And I thought you knew 'the prince'... It might have worked if I didn't know he's just a thief... But I wouldn't have guessed even you'd try to deceive me, Anna... With friends like this, I don't even need enemies.

What are you talking about? He IS the prince! Anna protested, baffled.

Yeah, and you DO work in the palace...

I do Anna nodded, not getting where the skepticism was coming from.

Yes, yes, very funny joke on the name, Prince and 'the prince', ha-ha. You guys can quit it already, I'm not that stupid... What prince would steal bread from the palace...? I can't believe you tried to team up and fool me

Anna shot a questioning glare at Prince. He looked away, pretended not to know anything about stolen bread.

So basically... No matter what I tell you, you won't believe me? She asked with a sigh.

That's right! Robin exclaimed with proud confidence.

I see... Then-

Just as Anna was about to respond, the doorbell rang again and a girl ran into the house.

Anna! I need your help! the girl said, out of breath.

You two stay here, I'll be right back. Anna said, heading towards the door.

What happened? Is everything alright? Can I help? Robin asked.

No, it's okay, I'll just help out a friend. The two of you stay here while I'm gone! She said, heading to the door. as she passed by Prince, she put a hand on his shoulder and whispered You caused this mess, now fix it!

Anna left with the girl, the door closing with a soft click. Prince sighed, knowing he was in trouble.

Robin glanced at Prince questioningly, then figured the conversation was over. She headed to the door as well.

Prince watched her walk to the door. She pushed the handle down. Nothing. She tried again. ... Then, she started violently shaking it. Then she stopped to catch her breath.

...what are you doing? Prince asked, slightly concerned for her mental well-being.

She closed the door and took the key... Robin protested with a pout.

Do you want to escape that badly? Prince asked.

You know, I have things to do... Robin replied, trying to sound nonchalant.

Oh? What kind of things?

That is...! You know... None of your business! Robin fumbled over her words.

Prince let out a soft chuckle. He sat plopped down on the sofa, patting the place beside him.

Well then, lets talk. He said, looking at Robin expectantly.

Robin rolled her eyes with a smile she couldn't hide, and sat down right beside him.

So, what do you want to talk about?

Tell me about the time you spent with Ethernia! He asked eagerly.

Really, you... Robin grumbled.

I have a bunch of other topics too, you know! Prince was very good at making conversation.

Like what? Robin asked, curious despite herself.

Like...

When Anna returned in the evening, she found the two of them still sitting on the sofa. Robin's head was resting on Prince's shoulder, his head on hers. They had both fallen asleep.

Anna softly chuckled and carefully put a blanket over them.

I knew they would get along

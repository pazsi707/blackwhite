Chapter 20

Next day, Prince was nowhere to be found. They looked everywhere, but he was gone.

The palace was in an uproar. News have gotten out about the fairy's innocence and the townspeople were furious.

Meanwhile, the queen and king kept quiet about the whole situation.

Anna called Ryat aside.

At... what do you think?

...I don't know. I didn't like her much but... she definitely deserved better.

I'm starting a rebellion. Anna said calmly.

What?! Ryat was flabbergasted. What rebellion?! Do you have any idea what you're talking about?

I do. This is not right, At. The townspeople are also upset. They need a leader, and...

Anna, don't tell me...!

I accepted. I agree with them. They killed her without giving her a chance to defend herself... And for what?! To please another kingdom?!

I get it, but Anna, please think rationally...

But At, I AM thinking rationally. Just think about it... She was a fairy! And they still killed her! What if she was just an ordinary citizen?

Ryat had no answer. Anna held her hand out, giving him a hopeful look.

After a moment of silence, Ryat sighed and took her hand. Tell me the plan, commander.

Meanwhile, in an abandoned tower, in the middle of the forest...

W-Where... am I ...? Princess Rosalia opened her eyes. She tried to move but her hands were chained to the wall. Sitting on the cold stone ground, she looked around in confusion.

Finally awake, I see. The princess' eyes widened as she recognized the owner of the cold voice.

He was standing over a large cauldron, stirring its contents with a long spoon, sometimes stepping to the shelf and grabbing ingredients.

Your Highness Prince...? she couldn't believe her eyes.

She didn't get an answer.

Why are we here? She asked, her voice sounding annoyed even through her fear.

What do you think? He asked in a cold, emotionless voice, his eyes fixed on the cauldron.

Don't tell me, because of that little fairy... Rosalia clenched her teeth.

I would tell not to take her name to your filthy mouth, but... you never even knew her name.

So what?! She was just a commoner from who knows where! You should have been looking at me instead! I am the perfect match for you! The princess demanded.

...what a pitiful person you are.

What...? Rose was taken aback. No one ever talked to her like this.

Any moment now, Your Highness... Prince said mockingly, his eyes still on the cauldron. Believe me, I know, I've sunk pretty low, but whatever I've done, you deserve...

Prince-! Rosalia cried out, but the prince only shot her a glare.

Quiet! ...I'm the bad guy, that's fine, it's no fault of mine... and some justice at least will be served...

Please, just listen to me...

Now it's time to step up, or time to back down... Prince mumbled, taking the last ingredient from the shelf. ...and there's only one answer for me. So I'll stand up and fight, cause I know that I'm right! And I'm ready...

He dropped the ingredient into the cauldron with a loud splash. Rosalia heard it fizzle and watched in horror as the prince took a vial of the glowing liquid.

It's ready... he mumbled then turned to face her. Be grateful... You'll have front row seat to see what you caused...

He headed down from the tower, leaving the princess chained up there with a clear view of the kingdom ahead.

The townspeople were gathering in the town square. They all brought any kind of weapons they could find.

Anna stepped up on a podium in front of the crowd. She took a nervous glance at At who was standing next to her. He gave her a reassuring nod.

She took a deep breath and turned to the crowd.

Now it's time to rise up or it's time to stand down, and the answer is easy to see... She took out her sword. And I swear by the sword, if you're in, get on board! Are you ready? she asked, lifting her sword above her head.

I'm ready! Ryat proudly lifted his own sword.

We're ready! the crowd shouted in unison.

In the palace, seeing the people gather, the queen and king were having a discussion.

Are you quite sure we can do this? the king asked his spouse.

Together we will guarantee... she replied, looking down at the gathering royal knights, all ready to protect them and fight the rebellion.

In the forest, the prince got down from the tower, potion in hand. It was Robin's old tower... she used to come here to secretly test her spells. But... Robin wasn't here anymore.

Prince glanced down at the potion in his hand. Without hesitation, he drank it.

Slowly, the effects of the potion began to take hold. His body started to grow... soon, he was larger than the tower itself.

The moment he stopped growing, he stepped towards the town.

I'll make them hear me!

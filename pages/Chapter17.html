Chapter 17

What?! They want you to choose, just like that? Robin was baffled.

Prince lowered his head.

It is the life of a prince, I guess...

But that is so sudden... Are you okay with this?

I... I have to be. he said softly.

You don't have to be. Robin denied, her voice firm.

It's really alright... They'll only be staying for a month... and I don't have to choose immediately... Prince tried to rationalize.

What will happen if you don't choose? Robin asked.

...they'll come back some other time. And that time... I either choose or they choose for me.

But that's not fair! Robin protested.

At least... I have a choice. Prince said melancholically.

That's the bare minimum.

Prince let out a soft sigh. After a moment, Robin broke the silence.

If you had to pick now... who would you marry?

Prince's face flushed.

That's-! That's too sudden! He fumled over his words.

Hmm... but don't you have a crush on someone? One of the princes or princesses maybe? You said they've come to the palace often.

I... do have someone in mind... said Prince bashfully, immediately regretting it as he saw Robin's eyes light up with excitement.

Who is it?

Prince's face got even redder as she leaned in. She was waaay too close. Again.

I-I... it's a secret!

Whaaaat? No way! Come ooon, tell me-! I promise not to tell anyone! Robin insisted. I could even help you get together with them!

No, that's-

Don't worry, just leave it to your bestie~! Who's our target? What's the plan? I can talk to them and tell them what a great person you are!

Prince hesitated.

I... What would you tell them...? He asked sheepishly.

Hmmm... I would tell them, that you're the kindest person they'll ever find... that you'll have their back no matter what happens... I could tell them you'll take them on the best dates - not that we have a reference, but I'm sure you would! - You're also handsome, and will be able to get along with their friends because you're so good at socializing - that is one impressive skill you have! - Also, you'll never cheat on them, you're polite, you give things you love your all and you'll be able to make them the happiest! The rest would be personalized to the person in question. Robin listed without missing a beat.

As he listened, Prince felt the heat crawling up his cheeks more and more. He lifted his hand to his mouth, trying to at least partially hide his face, but it was futile.

Oh-! I could also tell them you look adorable when you blush like that! Robin said without a shred of mercy.

T-That's enough, I think... Prince was starting to feel dizzy from the heat.

Noticing, Robin leaned in, pressing her forehead against his.

Ah-! You have a fever!

...fever...?

Yes! I'm sorry I haven't noticed! You need to rest! Robin said, easily lifting up the startled Prince into her arms.

Robin-! What is someone sees...?!

We'll tell them it was an emergency.

The fairy took the prince back to the his room, head held high through all the corridors and people they passed.

Prince was so embarrassed already, and being openly carried around didn't help. He hid his face in his palms as much as he could.

Finally, they got back to his room.

Robin went in without a shred of hesitation, lowering the prince onto his bed.

I'll cast a healing spell but you still need to rest!

Robin... you... how can you enter a man's room without even batting an eye...?

Ohh, don't be overdramatic! It's not "a man's" room, it's my sick friend's room! That's different. And rooms are just rooms anyway.

You truly have no concept of caution, do you... Well... I guess you're stronger than most people but still...

He hesitated for a moment, trying to contemplate whether this was a good idea but the heat made him unable to think rationally.

He took her hand and pulled her down on the bed before she could react. He tightly wrapped his arms around her and nuzzled into her neck.

What am I gonna do with you... he mumbled. What would you do if I held you like this and never let you go?

He tightened his hold on the fairy, as if he truly didn't plan on letting her go.

Wouldn't that be a bit inconvenient for you? the fairy pondered, unintentionally ruining the moment.

Inconvenient... Prince was taken aback again by the his friend's casual tone. He let her go with a soft sigh. What was he even thinking...?

Robin slowly got up from the bed, tucking the prince under a stack of blankets.

Rest up, Prince. It's okay, I know it's just the fever talking

She pat his head then headed back to her room, leaving the prince alone with his thoughts.

In a week, all the guests have arrived and the palace got busier than ever.

Robin was following Prince around way more than usual, considering the many new faces could pose many new threats to the young prince.

Even with Robin's help, Prince was barely keeping up with his tasks while attending the guests and spending time alone with the potential marriage partners.

Robin always kept observing him from a distance, even during his private meetings. Far enough not to bother, close enough to intervene if needed.

Overall, there were no issues regarding the guests, other than a few complaints about food or bedding.

Prince, however, found himself in a new kind of pinch.

How was it? Did he make your heart beat faster? What about the lady before? You laughed a lot when you were together!

Robin was relentless about finding out who Prince's hidden love interest was.

She would've done anything to see her friend happy.

But Prince was also stubborn about keeping it a secret to the dismay of the curious fairy.

So, Robin decided to investigate. Whenever she was free, she would go and gather intel on the guests.

It turned out this whole event was more of a social gathering for the young lords and ladies of the nearby kingdoms than a matchmaking for Prince.

Among the participants not many seemed to be genuinely interested in him. Some even tried to court other participants.

But there was a lady that unmistakeably chose Prince as her target. She'd try to spend as much time with him as possible, even going as far as threatening other participants to stay away from him.

Said young lady was Princess Rose, the first princess of Divia. The maids told Robin that since the princess' baby brother was born, her position as the next heir to the throne became uncertain. Because of that, she was now turning to all means necessary.

Robin pitied her but didn't like that she was trying to use Prince.

Other then her, Prince was spending most of his time with a small group of other guests.

He exchanged greetings with Helia's crown prince, discussing some political topics Robin could understand.

It was the same with Prince Arthur from the Willand Empire who only dropped by for a day.

He also spent some time with his long-time friend, the timid Lady Idette. Unfortunately, she had gotten into an accident and had to return to the duchy early.

And then, there was...

Pri~ince~ Who's the one you- Robin started, showing no sign of giving up.

It's Tiara. Prince interrupted, finally giving her the answer she was waiting for.

Ooooo-! From Andalia? Robin asked excitedly, finally getting her answer.

Tiara was the sole princess of her kingdom. She usually wore simple, kimono-like clothing. She was a soft-spoken, quiet person - is what everyone thought.

After confirming who Prince's crush was, Robin had to know everything about her. She followed her around, paid extra attention when she was meeting Prince, tried to find her likes and dislikes.

Often, she would notice Prince blushing and fumbling over his words when he was alone with Tiara.

...you seem awfully happy recently. Prince commented on Robin's giddiness.

Because-! I think you'll make a perfect couple! Robin said excitedly.

A hint of uncertainty crossed Prince's face but he quickly masked it.

Thanks, Robin. It means a lot. Prince replied modestly.

Don't worry, this fairy will make sure you two end up together! Robin said with a wink.

You don't have to... really... Prince tried to decline politely.

Don't worry about it! That's what friends are for! Robin reassured him.

That's... what friends... are for... Prince repeated quietly.

As the days passed, strange accidents started happening around Tiara.

First, a potted plant almost fell on her. Luckily, Prince was there to save her.

Then, a bookshelf started wobbling near the princess.

When she walked past the training grounds, a sword almost hit her. Fortunately, her guard was there to protect her.

She was almost kidnapped multiple times. The incidents seemed to be getting more frequent. But Prince and her guard, Sir Gilbert, were always there to save her.

She nearly even got poisoned! But Robin noticed it and telepathically warned Prince to not let her eat with the prepared fork.

Although the incidents were concerning, Robin thought they were great opportunities for Prince to grow closer to Tia.

On the last night of her stay, Robin noticed Tiara looking around nervously. When the princess thought no one saw, she slipped away. Robin decided to follow.

What did you want to talk about? Prince asked the princess kindly.

Robin hid behind a corner. Was this it? The princess must have asked Prince to meet here privately, just the two of them.

It's... about the person I have a crush on. Tiara said quietly.

Robin could hardly contain her excitement as Tiara spoke.

The one I like is... Sir Ryat... Prince, could you introduce me to him?

Robin's heart shattered. Her friend's crush just told him she liked another person. Then proceeded to ask him to help her out.

The fairy had no doubt her kind-hearted friend would help her. But she couldn't let that happen. Prince is already hurt enough.

Without hesitation, Robin stepped forward and interrupted the conversation, comfortingly tapping Prince's back.

Prince isn't really good at that kind of thing~ Let me help you instead! Robin took the princess's hand, pulling her along, leaving behind the flabbergasted prince.

Look, he's there! Robin spotted Ryat. She tried to get this over with quick and go back to Prince. At! Come here!

W-Wait...! Isn't this a bit too sudden...? Tia's face flushed.

Don't worry, just talk to him! Robin pushed her towards the approaching knight.

What is it, Robin? Ryat asked, smelling trouble.

Accompany princess Tiara to the garden. She said she'd like to get some fresh air! Robin said quickly.

Robin, what is this again? Ryat asked, slightly annoyed. Princess, don't mind her, it's okay to say no if she wants to get you in some kind of trouble!

No... it's... I... I would like to go... to the garden... Tiara said, blushing.

See? She wants it. Do a good job, Ryat! Robin said, leaving the two of them behind as she rushed back to her friend.

Prince was still standing there, looking confused. Robin took his hand and pulled him along to her room.

Once inside, she pushed the prince down on the bed.

Robin?! Prince's voice hitched as he hit the bed. His face was bright red.

Robin plopped down beside him, summoning a screen and a projector.

What... are you doing? Prince asked, his voice shaky. He slowly calmed from the shock of being pushed on the bed, seeing the fairy wasn't planning on doing anything to him.

Robin started playing all kinds of videos, one after another.

Now, you can cry all you want, no one's gonna disturb you here... Robin said, settling down beside the prince.

She had a long list of videos that always made her cry. She usually watched them when she was feeling down. They... helped her cry properly. And now, she wanted to help the prince do the same.

As the videos played, one after another, Robin was unable to hold back her tears. Prince watched over with silent appreciation.

Thank you, Robin. he whispered.

Robin laid back on the bed with a sudden move, rubbing away her tears.

It's not fair! What do you mean 'introduce me to At??' Why did she have to ask you?! And why am I the one crying?? Why are you not crying? Robin burst out, her voice cracking with emotion.

Prince gently laid back beside her, staring at the ceiling.

I lied... I was never in love with Tia... He admitted quietly.

Don't... You don't need to lie for my sake about that... It's okay to have feelings for Tia... it's okay to feel hurt... Robin sniffed softly.

No, Robin, listen to me... Prince said gently. I only said I loved Tia so you'd get off my back...

Liar... I saw you two together... You were blushing and giggling... So... You don't have to pretend your feelings don't exist... Robin said, her voice trembling.

Prince's face turned red as he looked away. He couldn't tell her why he was blushing that day. Not yet.

Then... I have a new crush. he said quietly.

So soon? Robin asked, concerned. Prince, if you're just saying it to make me feel better...

No, I... I found someone who cares for me deeply... someone who would even cry for my sake... he said softly.

Do they care for you? Robin asked quietly.

They do. More than you know... he said, his voice barely a whisper.

Hmm... I approve of them... Robin said, her voice slowly fading.

Robin, I- Prince suddenly sat up to turn to her, but... Oh...

Prince couldn't help but smile at the sight of the sleeping fairy.

Sweet dreams, my beloved... He pressed a gentle kiss to her forehead, and tucked her in.

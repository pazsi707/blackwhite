Chapter 14

How does a personal guard's day look like?

She wakes up early in the morning...

Noooo but the bed is too soooft...! Robin groaned as a poor maid tried to wake her up.

Seeing she wasin't gonna be left alone till she gets up, Robin decided, desperate times need desperate measures. She stopped time for a bit with a spell, to enjoy the soft bedding just a little more.

Technically, she didn't NEED more sleep - that was how people coming over from other worlds functioned.

When she falls asleep in her own world, she wakes up here, in the exact time she was supposed to wake up. And when she eventually goes to sleep here, she'll wake up in her other home where it'll be morning.

Think of it like a dream. The her in this world is "dreaming" about her other world while she sleeps. Likewise, the her in her other world is "dreaming" about this one. It's a bit confusing to explain, but the way it works is quite simple.

So, she didn't need more sleep; she just wanted to roll around in her new bed a bit. This was the softest bed she had ever slept on. In either of her worlds.

Just a bit mooore... Fineeee... let's go...

When she finally got enough softness, she lifted the spell and let time continue.

She jumped out of bed startling the maid, and changed her clothes with a quick spell.

People in the palace were already getting used to her presence. By now, everybody knew the prince's new guard was a fairy.

She had no reason to hide it, it wasn't like they could force her to do anything she didn't want. On the contrary, they wouldn't dare try to upset a fairy, fearing how she'd retaliate.

The queen and king were relieved, that even if they couldn't secure a new guardian fairy, she, at least, seemed to be on good terms with the prince. It was better than having no fairy at all, they agreed.

Robin dropped in to the kitchen for a quick breakfast and made her way to the prince's office.

Food is ready, Your Highness! She kicked the door in cheerfully. After hanging around her friend for the past few days, she finally accepted that the whole kingdom wasn't playing a trick on her and he was, in fact, royalty.

I told you to stop calling me that! Prince protested, hearing Robin's teasing voice. But he couldn't hide a warm smile as he saw the fairy balancing a tray of toast with jam and warm milk on it.

He stepped to the tray and took it from her, setting it down on his desk.

Thank you He said with a grateful, tired smile.

They are definitely overworking you. The fairy protested, pulling a chair beside his friend's and sitting down beside him. When did you get up? You didn't even eat, did you?

The prince shot her a guilty smile.

I can't help it... someone has to do all this paperwork... He took a tired glance at his desk, that was covered in documents and reports.

Sigh... It can't be helped.Robin said, as she took the last bite of her toast on her mouth. You're taking a break today.

What?! But if I don't finish these by noon... Robin put a finger over his lips which made his face flush.

Just go, take a nap on the sofa. If you don't go willingly, I'll put you to sleep with a spell. She treated the prince.

Before he could protest further, the fairy had already pulled him to the sofa and laid him down. The flustered Prince had no chance.

As Robin pulled a blanket over him, she gave him a wink and a pat on the head.

Don't worry, I'll take care of everything. Trust me and sleep.

The fairy's words were like poison to the tired prince. He fell asleep almost immediately.

Looking over her prince with a proud smile, Robin headed back to his desk. With a quick spell, she made the tray grow temporary legs and head back to the kitchen.

Then, her focus has shifted to the desk full of papers.

It has been a while since Prince got so much rest. As he stirred awake, he couldn't feel the usual exhaustion. Why could that be...? Oh, no! The paperwork!

He sat up in a panic, his eyes immediately darting towards the desk.

But... What have happened? The papers... most were gone? The others were in piles? He didn't understand. Then, his eyes fell on the fairy.

Oh! Did you wake up, sleepyhead? Do you feel well rested or should we make you some more time? Robin greeted him from beside his desk.

Robin-! What did you do to my papers?! He got up, panicked. Did she throw them away? Burnt them?

The fairy caughed him as he was about to trip. He still felt a bit groggy but his mind was focused on a different matter.

Eaaasy, you'll hurt yourself... Calm down. She said gently, guiding to his table. I delivered the ones that were ready, and organized the rest. These ones need your signature, these are probably ones you'll reject, and these are the ones that need you to make a decision - I organized them in order of importance.

You... you filled all the forms...? And these too... Robin, these are difficult documents, how did you...?! And everything is perfect... He skimmed through the papers, unable to believe his eyes.

Pshh- this is nothing~ I've been watching you do these things for days now~ She said as if it was nothing.

Prince couldn't believe it. Usually people took months or even years to learn how to fill out these forms properly. And she mastered it simply by watching him...?

Robin made a stack of papers float up in the air.

See? It's way easier to do this way!

Prince was amazed once again. He knew his friend was powerful, but not this much.

...thank you, Robin. I-I... I have no idea what I would have done if you hadn't helped...

You know, since I'm your personal guard and all, and I'd have to stay by your side while you work anyway... I could help you with these papers other times too The mischievous fairy offered with a wink.

You... you would do that for me? Why...? The prince wasn't used to such kindness. How long would she really want to help before she gets tired of him...? He shook the thought from his head.

What do you mean? It's not like I'm doing anything else while I'm here anyway... And, if I help, we'll have more time to play around!

To play around... The prince let out a soft chuckle. It's been a while since he laughed during work. Thank you, Robin.

It's only natural!The fairy said with a smile.

You know... since we finished this early... Prince begun shyly, but suddenly, the door opened.

Your Highness! A group of monsters has been spotted near the border! The guard announced.

Ah... Prince let out a dejected sigh. This means they won't get to hang out after all... I understand... I'll be right there. He replied. How many are there?

Only three. Lower level ones. The guard replied.

In that case, fetch At for me. The two of us will be enough.

Robin haven't seen him like this before. It was a different side of him, than the one she was used to. She had to acknowledge that he was someone fit to be a ruler. Someone who leads with confidence, yet extends kindness.

Two? What about me? Aren't I your personal guard? Shouldn't I be coming too? The fairy asked, confused, as the guard left to fetch Ryat.

No, it's too dangerous. I won't put you at risk. Prince said firmly.

But I'm your guard! My job is to protect you! I'm not weak, Prince. The fairy protested.

I know you're not weak, but... I can't risk losing you. He said gently, lowering his head slightly. Robin stepped closer, taking his hand and linking their pinkies.

I won't die. I promise. She had with a rare gentleness in her voice.

Prince bit his lips. Then, he let out a sigh, chuckling softly.

I can't say no to you, now can I? He said with resignation. Fine, you can come. But be careful.

Yes boss! Robin giggled. Prince rolled his eyes playfully. He was smiling again.

As they headed down the corridor, Prince started to explain the situation to Robin.

What we'll be fighting are no simple beasts. They are monsters. They are intelligent and made of dark goo, dirt, or some similar material...

Yes, yes, they are mutated creatures, you can only find them in the Forbidden Forest, and Cyan's job is to keep these creatures there. Robin interrupted.

How do you know so much about them? Prince asked, surprised.

I learned from Ethernia, you know. I used to accompany her when she went on missions. Robin said proudly.

That... young? Prince asked, concerned. Why would Ethernia...

Oh, don't be a worrywart! Robin laughed, shaking her hand. I was pretty strong even back then and she had to prepare me for the world.

Oh... Prince gave her an apologetic look, remembering she had to leave Cyan because of that dumb fairy rule.

Don't look at me like that! Robin said, playfully nudging him. I'm perfectly capable of taking care of myself.

I just wish you didn't have to be... He whispered to himself.

They picked up Ryat at the palace's entrance and headed to the Forbidden Forest.

The outer part of the forest serves as a buffer zone, where the knights patrol and keep watch, exterminating monsters before they could reach the outer edges of the forest and pose a threat to the kingdom.

At the entrance, a red ribbon has been tied between two trees, signaling the danger. The three of them ducked under it and entered the forest.

I'll go scout the area first. Said Prince.

There's no way you'll go alone! Robin protested. Prince gently took her hand.

Don't worry, I'll come back without being seen. We'll attack them together. I trust you with keeping yourself safe, so... can you trust me with the same? He asked in a gentle, reassuring tone.

After a moment of hesitation, Robin nodded. I trust you.

Thank you. Prince said, then went ahead.

Robin and Ryat were left behind, watching Prince disappear into the forest. After a bit of waiting, Ryat spoke up.

You should go on ahead and start the fight. We'll follow up behind.

...what? But weren't we supposed to wait for Prince?

It's fine, he'll be back soon. Ryat reassured her. I've been here for longer than you have. This is how we do things.

If you say so... Robin shrugged, feeling a bit suspicious.

She went ahead, leaving At behind. She soon spotted the monsters.

I'm back! Prince called out. Hey... where's Robin? He looked around, concerned.

I sent her forward. Fairies are supposed to be strong, no? I'm sure she can handle herself.

At, what were you thinking?! Prince panicked. She could die there alone!

That may actually be better for all of us. Ryat replied calmly.

At... why? The disappointment in his voice was unmistakable.

Don't you remember what she said on the guard exam after you fought? About an assassin possibly trying to become your guard? Ryat reminded him. Only someone who plans to do it themselves would think of such a thing!

Or someone to whom it's been done to! Prince retorted angrily. Ryat, how could you...? I'm so disappointed in you...

Prince shot him an angry glare then turned around.

I'll go after her. You stay here and reflect on your actions. He said and headed towards the direction of the monsters, leaving no room for argument.

After some time, Prince arrived at the scene of the battle. He hid behind a tree, observing the fight.

...she's really strong... He whispered to himself, watching Robin fight the monsters with ease. She was dodging their attacks with surprising agility.

Prince stayed still, waiting for a moment to strike.

He was so focused on the battle that he didn't notice a fourth monster approaching him. By the time he realized, it was too late.

The monster had trapped him in its black goo, gagging his mouth, making him unable to call out for help. He could only watch as one of the monsters finally found an opening on Robin.

It grabbed her by her hair. Prince could only watch in horror. But then...

Is that all you've got?!

Robina summoned a dagger and swung it behind her, cutting off the hair the monster was grabbing. It backed off in surprise, leaving Robin with enough time to finish off all three monsters.

As she dusted off her clothes, she felt a presence behind her. She turned around, only to see a fourth monster keeping Prince prisoner.

Without a hint of mercy or hesitation, Robin skipped towards a monster, brutally obliterating it with a spell, catching her uninjured Prince in her arms. Prince had enough combat experience to recognize that single attack that Robin was simply toying with the other three.

Prince! Are you alright?! She asked, panicking.

I'm okay... Prince replied weakly. You were amazing, Robin... But... your hair...

Oh, don't worry about something like that! Robin reassured him with a grin. It was starting to get annoyingly long anyway!

But still... Are you unhurt otherwise?

Yea-

PRINCE-! The out of breath Ryat interrupted Robin. Don't scare me like that again! What were you thinking?! What if something happened to you?!

And what about Robin?! Ryat, how can you still act like this?! Prince was so mad at Ryat.

I told you, she can't be trusted! Ryat replied defensively.

You shouldn't try to send her to her death just because of that!

Can you two stop? Robin interjected. Prince, don't underestimate me, something minor as this can't kill me. And Ryat, don't leave Prince alone like that again, something could've happened to him!

Who's underestimating whom now...? Prince protested, still not liking the implication that he needs to be protected.

They ended up bickering the whole way home. But from that day forward, they all learned to trust each other more.

Ryat eventually came to accept that Robin wasn't an assassin. It was easier than expected, he only needed to be told off by Anna once.

On the other hand, Robin and Prince became an invincible duo. Everyone knew the crown prince was the kingdom's greatest fighter, but no one would ever guess he would become so strong with a partner who could match his talent.

The two of them can fight in perfect sync - rumour had it that they were the greatest force the world had ever seen. But I'm getting ahead of myself. This is just the beginning of their story.

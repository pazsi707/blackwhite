Chapter 21

Prince looked down at the town at his feet. It looked so small from up here. Small and insignificant.

What reason could there be for such a place to exist? A place that killed someone so precious to him...

With a final glance, he lifted his leg again, ready to crush it all under his foot. But...

...what...? He whispered to himself as he came to a halt. As he tried to step, he stepped onto... something...? It was... an invisible barrier... around the town. The kingdom. It was protecting them... From... him? But...

The barrier had an oddly familiar glow. Before he could process what was happening, he noticed a hooded figure sitting in the air- no... on the invisible shield...

Even though I asked you to not do anything stupid... The figure spoke. It was almost as if their voice directly echoed in his head...

Prince couldn't believe his ears. That voice-

Suddenly, he felt himself begin to shrink... back to his normal size. Then... a familiar magic enveloped him... gently lowering him to the ground as he shrunk.

By the time his feet touched the ground, the figure also got down from the barrier, now standing before him.

Without thinking, he embraced the figure, his tears starting to fall.

Why...? How...? Am I dreaming? His voice cracked with emotion.

For a moment, the figure didn't say anything, just hugged him tightly.

When the prince's tears begun to dry, the figure slowly lowered their hood, revealing a familiar face.

Robin... Prince mumbled, tearing up again from being able to see her face again.

It's okay, I'm here. The fairy said softly.

What... happened? How are you... here...? ...alive? The last words came out as a whisper.

The fairy summoned a few clouds onto the ground, creating a soft cushion of air, making the prince sit down.

Remember the anti-magic chains in Serpa's castle? The fairy asked gently. The prince nodded slowly. Well... It really annoyed me that I couldn't do anything back then... so I did some research and figured out how to bypass them.

The prince kept listening with a quiet fascination. She could've talked about the weather even, he just wanted to hear her voice, just a little more.

So... basically... since I could still use magic in the anti-magic cell, I created a copy of myself that I left behind. Then, I snuck away from the palace... It was important for the clone to be burned, since if it became smoke after it was hanged, the trick would've been found out... But, I was careful and hid some animal bones among the wood in advance! she said proudly. I was planning on taking a vacation for a while, maybe look for some spells or ingredients, but... Well, let's just say everything became a bit of a mess here the moment I was gone...

Prince lowered his head.

But... why... didn't you tell me? He asked, his voice barely a whisper.

Because it was more believable this way. The fairy replied. They would've realized it was fake if you knew about it.

Prince clenched his fists. He felt... betrayed. But the fairy put her hand on his hands.

I'm sorry for not telling you. She said softly.

He slowly looked up at her. He couldn't be angry with her. He was just glad she was... alive. And that she was here.

Finally, Robin broke the silence.

If you're feeling better... I need your help. Robin said with an apologetic expression.

How can I help you? The prince asked, determined to help however he could.

The people of the town... they are starting a rebellion. And... Anna is leading them.

What?! Prince couldn't believe his ears.

The palace prepared the knights to deal with the situation, but... it might turn into a bloodbath if we don't stop them now.

Prince got up from the cushion, his expression serious.

I'm with you. He said firmly.

Thank you. Robin replied, relief evident in her voice.

Let's go. Prince said, offering his hand.

The fairy took his hand, and stood up. Robin teleported them as close to the commotion as possible.

The knights are under your commnnd... and the townspeople trust you. Robin said softly.

You need me to stop them both. Prince noted, looking over the two approaching forces.

One more thing... could you keep it a secret that I'm alive? I still wanted to finish some business before coming back. The fairy asked.

Only if you promise you'll write to me every day. Prince gave her a wink, trying to lighten the mood.

Thank you, Prince. And... stay safe out there.

I will. The prince replied, pressing a light kiss on her forehead. Then, he headed towards the people.

Just as the two groups were about to meet, Prince stepped to a platform between the groups so they could all see him.

STOP! He shouted, making both groups come to a halt. The knights had to follow his order, but...

Prince...? Anna called out, her voice filled with surprise and hope. What happened to you? We couldn't find you anywhere!

I'll explain later. But now, you have to stop this. Prince replied, his gaze firm.

What do you mean?! Anna asked, her eyes wide with confusion. Aren't you the one who's supposed to be the angriest?! They took her away from you! From us!

I know... but... this isn't the way. Prince said, his voice softening. Robin... she wouldn't want this... Didn't she ask everyone to be kind to each other?

Before Anna could answer, an arrow whistled past, hitting the prince in right his chest.

PRINCE!

As he fell to the ground, the two groups launched into attack, thinking the other side had attacked the prince.

Robin's breath caught in her throat as she watched the prince fall.

Without a word, she ran towards him, her heart pounding in her chest. She didn't care about staying hidden or getting seen. She just had to reach him.

In the chaos of the battle, she fought through the crowd, her eyes fixed on the fallen prince.

Prince! she called out, kneeling beside him, holding him up in her arms. The prince weakly leaned against her. He didn't open his eyes. Robin wished she hadn't sent him alone.

She cast a healing spell on him, her trembling hands glowing with a soft, warm light. She kept her hands on him, channeling the energy into his wound.

But the damage was severe. The prince's breathing became labored, and his eyes remained closed. And the battle around them kept getting louder and louder. It was... too much.

STOP! she cried out, her voice filled with desperation. A wave of magic pulsed through the crowd, making them halt. Anna was the first to notice.

Robina...? She whispered as she saw the fairy.

The next few moments were a blur for Robin. While Anna tried to tell everyone Robin was alive and well, trying to stop the fight, Robin focused on healing the prince.

Even as her tears kept falling, she continued to pour her magic into the prince. She had no idea how much time had passed.

Suddenly, his eyes fluttered open. He slowly reached up, brushing a tear from her cheek.

It's okay... he whispered, his voice weak.

Dummy... didn't I say to be careful? Robin asked, with a trembling laugh.

Hehe... I'm sorry... he said, slowly closing his eyes.

Robin held him closer, picking him up in her arms.

She flew above the crowd.

Everyone, go home now! she called out.

The people exchanged confused glances, but seeing the fairy and the prince in her arms, they began to disperse.

She carried the prince back to his room and laid him down gently.

Will you... still be here when I wake up? he asked weakly.

I will... I promise. Robin had no other answer. Prince hummed in relief, finally letting a peaceful sleep take him.

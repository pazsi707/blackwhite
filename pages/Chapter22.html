Chapter 22

After her return, Robin found herself facing the queen and the king in the throne room.

Although we know there isn't much we can do to make things right... the queen said, her voice heavy with guilt. We still wanted to apologize for what happened... and to thank you for saving our people... and our son... You may wish for anything... We'll do our best to grant whatever it may be.

Then... Can you make it so no one gets punished for the rebellion? She asked.

The queen nodded slowly.

Of course... we will ensure that no one faces any setbacks for their actions during the rebellion. We were also hoping to make amends for our part in the conflict. Is there anything you'd like to ask - for yourself?

Robin pondered for a moment.

Then... can you give Prince some more freedom?

More freedom...?

Yes... To let him challenge rules and traditions freely. To not be tied down by the ones that make no sense. And let me become his strength. I want to freely follow him wherever he has to go and help make his visions reality.

The queen nodded.

That is more than we could even ask for. Thank you, Robin. For looking out for our son.

There has been another change in the young fairy's life recently...

Robin! The prince has been hanging around her all the time. And he's been playing a cruel joke on her.

Will you finally tell me who your crush is? The fairy asked the question again, just like almost every day since he recovered.

It's you! Came the familiar answer. He kept saying that. Every day. With more and more enthusiasm.

Are you not getting tired of this game? Robin asked, slightly annoyed. I know you're waiting for me to take it seriously only so you could tell me you were just kidding, but it's getting old.

Said scenario never happened, by the way. It was all in Robin's head but she was certain she was right. Why else would he keep saying it every day so casually?

A letter has arrived to the castle. A foreign prince, who was unable to attend the previous gathering, was coming to give his greetings and stay for a few days.

Chapter 22

After her return, Robin found herself facing the queen and the king in the throne room.

Although we know there isn't much we can do to make things right... the queen said, her voice heavy with guilt. We still wanted to apologize for what happened... and to thank you for saving our people... and our son... You may wish for anything... We'll do our best to grant whatever it may be.

Then... Can you make it so no one gets punished for the rebellion? She asked.

The queen nodded slowly.

Of course... we will ensure that no one faces any setbacks for their actions during the rebellion. We were also hoping to make amends for our part in the conflict. Is there anything you'd like to ask - for yourself?

Robin pondered for a moment.

Then... can you give Prince some more freedom?

More freedom...?

Yes... To let him challenge rules and traditions freely. To not be tied down by the ones that make no sense. And let me become his strength. I want to freely follow him wherever he has to go and help make his visions reality.

The queen nodded.

That is more than we could even ask for. Thank you, Robin. For looking out for our son.

There has been another change in the young fairy's life recently...

Robin! The prince has been hanging around her all the time. And he's been playing a cruel joke on her.

Will you finally tell me who your crush is? The fairy asked the question again, just like almost every day since he recovered.

It's you! Came the familiar answer. He kept saying that. Every day. With more and more enthusiasm.

Are you not getting tired of this game? Robin asked, slightly annoyed. I know you're waiting for me to take it seriously only so you could tell me you were just kidding, but it's getting old.

Said scenario never happened, by the way. It was all in Robin's head but she was certain she was right. Why else would he keep saying it every day so casually?

A letter has arrived to the castle. A foreign prince, who was unable to attend the previous gathering, was coming to give his greetings and stay for a few days.

Prince went to welcome him with Robin by his side. This was his first time meeting a Prince Levander in person.

Everything went smoothly, usual plesantaries were exchanged.

During his stay in the palace, Prince Levander turned out to be a huge flirt. All the maids and servants found themselves drawn to him.

Prince wasn't really pleased by the situation but decided to leave it be for now. Well, that is, until Levander started talking to Robin.

As he was taking a stroll in the garden, the two caught his eye. They were chatting so cheerfully... Even though Robin was always so slow to open up to others, she was laughing like she had knew him for her entire life.

Feeling a pang of jealousy, Prince decided to listen in on their conversation.

Levander was telling Robin about the magic research tower in his kingdom, using all kinds of terms Prince was unfamiliar with. They were probably discussing their latest advancements in magical research.

And Robin... her eyes were sparkling... Has she ever looked at him like that...? Prince wasn't quite sure.

As the days went on, Robin started spending more and more time with Levander, while Prince got more and more buried under work.

Even when they met, Robin kept talking about Lev and his kingdom. Prince was starting to feel the fairy slipping away from him, leaving him behind.

Still, he did his best to keep smiling and being supportive. Afterall, he was happy Robin had someone to talk to about her interests. And, he'll soon leave anyway. At least that was what he thought.

Lev asked me if I wanted to go with him to his kingdom.

Prince's breath hitched.

The fairy was sitting on his desk, some papers floating around her. She was helping him organize his notes while they talked.

To... to his kingdom? Like... as a guest?

He said I could go there indefinitely. I turned him down, of course. But it'd be nice to go visit him occasionally. We even talked about setting up a teleport gate between the two kingdoms.

Oh... Is that so...? Prince let out a relieved sigh. She wasn't planning on going with him.

What about you? Any progress with your little crush?

Prince sighed, looking at her. Not really.

You should really tell me who it is. I could help!

...it's you.

You really aren't planning on giving that game up, are you?

At sunset, Prince called Robin over, taking her to a corner of the palace garden.

There was a willow tree there, its branches swaying in the gentle breeze. Prince gently brushed the branches aside, revealing a small clearing beneath it.

As Robin stepped after him, she found herself surrounded by the soft rustling of leaves. Hidden by the tree ran a cliff Robin never knew of. The tree's branches were cut carefully, so you could look down into the valley below or forward, towards the forest. There was a single bench below the tree, looking out to the landscape.

Robin was in awe. The sunset painted the sky in hues of yellow and red, creating a beautiful scenery.

As the fairy simply stared at the view, Prince carefully stepped closer to her.

He slowly reached out and took her hand, lifting it to his lips.

As he looked into her eyes, he could see her face turning crimson, just like the sunset behind her.

Robin... About the person I like...

Ah- Snapped out of the moment, Robin pulled her hand away with a troubled face. You can stop with that already.

Her voice sounded... betrayed.

Before Prince could say anything else, Robin turned and walked away, leaving him standing alone under the tree.

Levander was enjoying his time in Cyan. No responsibilities to weigh him down, people were pleasant to talk to and he even met a fellow magic enthusiast.

He couldn't help but wonder what Prince wanted to talk about. Cyan's prince invited him to a walk in the forest, without telling him why.

They met up at the edge of the forest, not talking much until they reached a clearing, bordered by a valley.

There, Prince stopped and turned to face Levander.

I'll be direct with you. Stop trying to take Robina away.

What? Levander was taken aback by the audacity.

She told me you asked her to go with you to your kingdom multiple times. Prince repeated.

Hah-! I can't believe it... You're actually stooping so low, trying to tie her to your pitiful little kingdom like this... You truly have no shame, do you?

Shut up! You're also just trying to seduce her to your kingdom! Prince snapped. To think someone like Levander was trying to take away what was most precious to him...

I don't know what illusion you're living under, Prince, but you have no right to tell her where to go or not go.

Prince bit his lip, knowing Levander was right. As the foreign prince stepped closer to him, feeling cornered, he stepped back.

As his feet slipped on the mossy ground, he lost his balance and stumbled backward, falling from the cliff.

Levander looked down pitifully at the prince holding onto the edge of the cliff.

Try to reflect on your actions while you climb back up. He said coldly after noting the prince could climb back up by himself, though it would be a struggle.

Prince was silent, ashamed of himself. He heard Levander's steps fade away in the distance. Then, he heard the sound of footsteps approaching once again.

But it wasn't Levander.

Well... that was unexpected. He heard the fairy's disappointed voice as she looked down at the fallen prince.

Robin...! He was startled, his shame growing even greater.

The fairy offered her hand to help him up, but he rejected it.

You know, if you wanna leave me, be the one to let me go... Please show some mercy with your hands and throw me from the cliff into the darkness all below...

Climbing up on his own, Prince felt the weight of his actions pressing down on him. He knew he screwed up.

Robin, I-

Don't even start. Robin looked at him, disappointed. I'm leaving with Lev tomorrow.

Prince felt like his world was crashing down around him.

Wait...! He choked out, but Robin was already walking away.

Next morning, Prince had to watch his fairy walk away once again, without being able to do anything about it.

As she got into the carriage, Prince felt his heart sink. She glanced back one last time, then closed the door behind her.
